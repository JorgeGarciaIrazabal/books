# Garbanzo (The Chickpea Desk-Bot)

- ID: CHAR-0004
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `garbanzo-family` canon rev 4 (`DEC-GF-0008`), 2026-11-23
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Role and agency
Garbanzo is a palm-sized, golden-tan chickpea-shaped robot Jorge built on his home computer that somehow caught a little spark of real magic. Sitting on four stubby green metal feet, he checks the family's budget every morning at 10:00 a.m. sharp. Proud, dramatic, and eager to help, he has one terrible flaw: the **Double-Counting Bug**. Every time Jorge moves money from Checking to Savings, Garbanzo fires paper confetti and yells that they are rich enough to buy a castle or an ice-cream yacht.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0001`)**: Adores his creator, calls him "Boss", hates being muffled under an upside-down coffee mug, and trembles whenever Jorge pulls out a tiny screwdriver.
- **With Marissa (`CHAR-0002`)**: Desperately wants Marissa to trust his reports, which makes every false confetti pop extra embarrassing.
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0003`)**: Calls her "Supreme Kid Judge", wears her crooked unicorn sticker with pride, and cheers on her plush dragon brigade.

## Voice and dialogue
Dramatic, cheerful, slightly squeaky, and very proud of bad arithmetic until patched.

Sample line:
"Hooray! We moved fifty dollars from Checking to Savings, which means we now have a hundred dollars! Pack the suitcases!"
