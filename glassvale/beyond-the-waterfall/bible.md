# Beyond the Waterfall: Series Bible

## Series Promise
Five connected, richly illustrated adventures following Clara from age eleven to fifteen at Glassvale Academy. Each book delivers a complete, thrilling mystery on its own while peeling back one layer of a valley-wide plot: **The Hollow Circle** is quietly racing to reassemble the five lost pieces of **The World-Glass**.

## Core Theme
No single gift is strong enough alone. Pure Magic burns brightest and safest only when paired with a Maker's patience and Nature's steady balance.

## Escalation Across Five Books
1. **Age 11 (Book 1):** Discovering the three Paths, earning trust across teachers and friends, and defending the Academy's Bell-Vault from a single intruder.
2. **Age 12 (Book 2):** Stepping beyond the school gates into the **Copper Peaks** sky-workshops, where a stolen Maker Chamber tests Clara's friendship with Jorge and Sam.
3. **Age 13 (Book 3):** Journeying deep into the **Whisperwood**, where ancient living roots guard the Nature Chamber and Maya faces a painful choice about wild freedom vs. loyalty.
4. **Age 14 (Book 4):** Crossing the cold **Ash Marsh** as Shadow Magic leaks across Glassvale and the Starlight Chamber falls into enemy hands, forcing Victor to choose which side he truly stands on.
5. **Age 15 (Book 5):** Inside the hidden **First Vault** beneath the Mistgate waterfall, the shocking reveal that **Peter** has been guiding The Hollow Circle from the beginning, and the final stand where Clara, Maya, Sam, Jorge, Marissa, and Monica unite all three Paths to save Glassvale.
