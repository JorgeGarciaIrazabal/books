# Master Peter

- ID: CHAR-0004
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glasshaven` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GH-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 through Book 5

## Role and agency (Public / Book 1 View)
Master Peter (early forties) is the Leader Teacher of the Path of Stars (Pure Magic). To students and colleagues alike, he seems like the steady, generous heart of the Star Spire: a towering presence who laughs easily, shields frightened first-years, and makes Pure Magic feel thrilling rather than scary.

## Author/Series Secret (NOT Book 1 POV or Reader Knowledge)
- **Strict Spoiler Guard (`FACT-0010`, `DISC-0001`)**: Master Peter is secretly aligned with the **Quiet Crown** faction. Across Books 1 to 5, he uses his genuine charm, high trust, and mentorship of gifted Star-students like Clara to locate and gather the **Threefold Keys** to the Heart-Vault. In Book 1, he must remain completely warm, heroic, and trustworthy on the surface, with only tiny, fair-play inconsistencies (such as arriving suspiciously fast or quietly pocketing a clue after the dust settles) that only make full sense in Book 5.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0001`)**: Warm, encouraging mentor who gives Clara her silver Star-Pin and praises her courage.
- **With Nora (`CHAR-0008`)**: Smoothly firm on the surface; quietly steers her ambition without exposing himself.
- **With Master Jorge (`CHAR-0002`) & Master Marissa (`CHAR-0003`)**: Cordial fellow council member who uses politeness to keep them out of the Star Spire's lowest vaults.

## Voice and dialogue
Rich, reassuring, unhurried, and comforting, with genuine warmth in his delivery.

Sample line:
"Breathe easy, little star. Power feels wild the first time it answers your hand, like a big dog pulling on a leash. You do not need to fear it. You just need to hold steady."
