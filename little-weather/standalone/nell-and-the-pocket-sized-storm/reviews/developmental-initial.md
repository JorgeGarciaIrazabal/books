## Revision letter

The story delivers its promise. Preserve the boot opening, Grumble's indignant thirst defense, the invitation to choose the beat, and the damp mitten after the goodbye. They give the fantasy an identity and let tenderness arrive through objects and behavior.

Causality holds across the whole story. Pages 1–4 establish Nell's curiosity, the separated family, and the sunset deadline. Pages 5–10 supply a failed launch, waiting time, accidental enlargement, and the need to rain. The drinking complication arises from Nell's practical decision to leave Grumble beside the tap rather than from an unrelated interruption. Pages 11–20 make the solution depend on repairing trust: Nell laughs, apologizes without demanding forgiveness, offers accompaniment, and gives Grumble control. The widening band waits for his consent on page 16. His own thunder then releases the water. Keep this sequence intact.

Pages 21–27 combine the returning breeze, the moving family, the kite, and the familiar rhythm. Grumble chooses the jump; Nell supports him without physically solving it. The family answering in different sounds is an especially effective payoff. It complicates his belief that his thunder is "wrong" without inserting a corrective lecture. The family need not be explained further.

Timing is legible: Grandma identifies the expected evening breeze on page 7; the pink sky and nearly reached hilltops on page 21 make the final opportunity visible. The knot delays the launch briefly and gives Grandma a useful part. There is no need for a countdown or additional peril. Pages 28–30 allow separation, bedtime reassurance, and a final visual joke. The rainbow is a harmless new magical flourish, not an unearned solution to the central problem.

Read-aloud pacing suits the intended shared reading: short exchanges, repeatable percussion, a quiet pause before Grumble's confession, and distinct action beats at launch. Pages 15–20 sustain the band through changes in participation, confidence, and destination. I would retain their repetitions. Page 28's quieter damp-thumb image earns the transition to bedtime. No major compression is warranted.
