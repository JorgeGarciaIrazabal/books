# Marissa, Royal Inquisitor

- ID: CHAR-0001
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `corporate-castle` canon rev 1 (`FACT-0002`, `FACT-0005`, `FACT-0009`; Google Doc Tab 4), 2026-11-25
- Story-time validity: Prologue opening (baseline); chronological arc tracked in Book 1 `scenes.json` and `knowledge.json`

## Role and agency
- **Role**: Royal Inquisitor of the ER Watch, licensed practitioner of Deep Magic, and keeper of Corporate Castle's highest-stakes Formal Inquiries into Crimes Against the Codex (discrimination, harassment, and Crown-Level landmines) inside the ER Vault. Reluctant translator and referee between Liability Tower and the Courtiers of Optics on messy High Lord reorgs, Headcount Pruning, and High Lord Okta Excommunications (`FACT-0009`).
- **Want**: To practice Deep Magic cleanly in service of truth and the people doing the actual work, with clear boundaries, real operational support, and leadership that backs the Codex regardless of rank.
- **Values & capacity**: Possesses both formal licensing (her own heavily tabbed Spellbooks of Deep Magic) and the innate gift (True-Sight, HR Lore, The Velvet Snare, The Credibility Scales, and her signature Mirror Shield).
- **Limits**: Bound by the Two-Way Law of Free Will (`FACT-0006`): she cannot force a Warlord, High Lord, or Archivist to do the right thing, only expose the truth and leave them holding ownership—and, when faith breaks completely, exercise her own free will to walk out the gates.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Doctor Lois (`CHAR-0002`)**: Starts in genuine, effortless Deep Magic sync on ordinary risk, respecting Lois's frostbite and lack of pastel theater. That respect collapses completely once Lois refuses to spend political capital fighting Sterling for Watch budget and folds upward on the Bullying High Lord inquiry.
- **With Sarai (`CHAR-0003`)**: True peer, friend, and co-carrier of the ER Watch after Lexi's departure. Marissa steps up to cover Sarai's load out of loyalty to Sarai and the Squires, until the unfunded "temporary" load becomes a permanent trap.
- **With High Lady Lexi (`CHAR-0004`)**: Former leader and protector; Marissa chose pure Deep Magic while Lexi chose the High Lord climb.
- **With Riley (`CHAR-0009`) and Victoria (`CHAR-0010`)**: Deeply protective of the frontline Squires, even though managing frontline PIPs and Writs of Warning is not her calling.
- **With Sir Leo (`CHAR-0007`)**: Friend, confidant, and fellow licensed practitioner of Deep Magic whose dry wit she enjoys and whom she trusts when stakes get real. Carries a quiet undercurrent of irritation whenever Leo vents out-of-touch complaints about Lois's frostiness from inside Liability Tower or quietly leans on Marissa's heavy lifting—yet accepts that as the unspoken tax of his allyship because when it counts (like the Bullying High Lord inquiry), he goes to bat for her and takes the hit.
- **With Baroness Orla (`CHAR-0006`, proposed)**: Utterly immune to Orla's Buzzword Hypnosis and Illusion Magic; punctures spin with single sentences of dry operational truth.

## Voice and dialogue
Calm, clinical, unhurried, and allergic to theater. Sir Leo describes her interview style as **"like a knife through butter"**: a velvet glove hiding an iron fist. Usually mild and disarming, letting subjects wade deeper into their own lies while strictly protecting witness confidentiality (testing reported concerns without ever naming who said what). What truly triggers her controlled razor edge is a condescending High Lord treating her like a Courtier or trying to wave Illusion Magic past her as though he is the smartest person in the room. She never argues or pre-judges; instead she deploys the deadliest spell in her arsenal—completely neutral, bulletproof under Deep Magic, and grounded in free will: *"I have received the concern that you [violation]. This is your opportunity to provide your perspective. If you choose not to respond, I will make my determination based on the information I have."* When deploying **The Mirror Shield**, she offers stony, unblinking stillness and one clean operational question that strips away spin.

Sample line (grounded in Tab 4 canon):
"If Orla isn't backfilling Sarai's seat, everything continues to fall on me."
