# Working character and material continuity

Based on chapters 1–2; later arcs are provisional.

- Ragna: accountant, knew Dorrik professionally for sixteen years, values signed authority and fair procedure. She now holds her desk key and a copy of the fragment. She has begun to see how little she knows of the households behind the figures.
- Tavin: nineteen, six months an apprentice, fast at arithmetic. He wants to distribute available money sooner. His mother cuts his hair; her other circumstances are open.
- Sella: Dorrik's wife, first name on the workshop plate, maker and accurate bookkeeper. She negotiates credit, refuses the ambiguous release and holds the original fragment and body receipt. Paying her workers is a priority distinct from the investigation.
- Neri: Dorrik's daughter, hinge-maker, designs the household warning bell and counted the iron. Has a commission to fit a latch at Blackwater; distrust of Ragna follows the disputed accounts.
- Orren: their son, sorts rivets and wants materials for a boat. Do not repeatedly use him as a convenient source of decisive clues.
- Dorrik: fitter and delivery worker, skilled with gates, poor at mending his own cup. His circumstances of death are unknown to the viewpoint.
- Brann: porter from the lower furnaces, brought the body and said Dorrik fell; his motives and knowledge are not yet established.
- Ilva, Merrok, Harrow: their public actions and positions are established; the private motives in plot-engine.md remain unrevealed to the characters and reader.

Numbers: twenty bars received according to Sella, forty on a note signed by Dorrik according to Ragna's memory, and two separate charges of forty in the ledger. Each needs separate explanation and evidence. The moulding-sand supplier can wait for payment until Friday but cannot extend further credit. Different workers have made different arrangements. A school commission is established; the institution is not named. Keep objects, food, humour and magic particular to individuals and work, not uniform cultural rules.

## Through chapter 10

Vessa is Brann's twin sister and a chain tester; she and Brann moved to Tavin's mother's house after deciding to leave works housing. Fenn is a glassworker and one of three mutual-fund trustees with Sella and Vessa. He compared Venn's dispatch copies with the original; his right wrist is injured in the weigh-house confrontation. Edda works at the small furnace; she accepted Merrok's offer, has an aunt omitted from the dependants register, and her lantern and cheek were damaged at the confrontation. Brelk accepted conditional relief to pay for a warm room for his coughing son, and gave to the independent fund.

Daniel Venn is a Ministry dispatch clerk who admits changing the destination of the second twenty bars under instruction through Harrow's office. His mother independently chose to move in with his sister Ruth; his aunt owns the paper shop. Venn authorised use of the dispatch facts and his name in the inquiry but not posting his name on a wall. He surrendered his original dispatch to Harrow and was detained. Ilva certified the reserve advance and concealed its reconciliation; she has acknowledged these actions but her fuller evidence still needs corroboration. Issa and Halrik are the partners who can examine balances; Arven, senior bank clerk, retains original working slips and certified copies for Ragna. Harrow and Pell took Venn and Tavin toward Blackwater after the incident; Reed is another wizard officer who used a clearing spell and then sought help for an injured man.
