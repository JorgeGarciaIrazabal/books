# Observed illustration and production lessons

2026-10-25. This is a record of actual work on Nell, not model training or a claim that a single project proves a universal method.

| Observation and evidence | Intervention and recheck | Reusable scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original 30-page story had one source file, so “per chapter” had no useful chapter structure. The archived original and new book.json identify the before/after. | Make six meaningful five-page chapters; compare all 30 page bodies to archived v1. Exact story prose is unchanged. | Count actual narrative chapters separately from files, scenes and pages; preserve author's length. Six chapters is specific to this book. |
| The shared cast sheet's dog carried into ILL-0003 on page 6 before its page-16 entrance. Rejected version is assets/iterations/ill-0003-before-dog-removal.png. | A localized image-tool edit removed the dog; coordinator viewed the repaired original and final page. | State included and excluded cast per scene. A shared reference is not a populated-scene instruction. |
| ILL-0007 rendered two cloudpoints with extra complete faces and omitted band instruments. Independent review identified the same faults as coordinator inspection. | Remove duplicate faces, restore visible instruments, retain original composition. Repaired asset and actual page 17 inspected. | Check the literal reading of creature anatomy and active props, not only style. “No human limbs” alone does not prevent duplicate creatures. |
| ILL-0011 omitted the kite tether/winder even though the next page recovers the kite. | Add one fine tether and winder in Nell's free hand; preserve spoon and reunion. Repaired asset and page 27 inspected. | Include possession and object custody across adjacent scenes in visual proof. |
| Plan's 3:2 source at height 2.5 in is width 3.75 in, not width 5.9 in. | Preserve complete image at 3.75 × 2.5; no stretch/crop. Browser and rendered pages verify fit. | Compute actual aspect geometry; don't assume a wide maximum box is the placed image. This trim is not a universal standard. |
| First illustrated builder produced a PDF but its asynchronous load/decode proof report remained `{}`. Build was rejected by its own check. | Measure synchronously after embedded image load; require all 30 frame measurements and 12 decoded images, then inspect actual PDF. | A created PDF is insufficient evidence that readiness/geometry checks ran. Use a real completed-render predicate. |
| Lossless PNG embedding produced roughly 50 MB, despite a small chapter-book page image size. | Preserve original 1536 × 1024 PNGs; use JPEG 92 4:4:4 derivatives at unchanged dimensions for this digital edition. Final PDF is about 8.8 MB; final page images inspected. | Derive placement assets deliberately; keep masters and record actual reproduction choices. Do not impose a printer threshold on digital reading. |
| ILL-0004's provisional alt text described eyebrows over handles that the final art did not show. | Correct alt to actual enormous cloud with Nell/Grandma; final geometry and all page bodies unchanged after metadata-only rebuild. | Write useful initial alt, then revise against accepted output. HTML alt does not certify PDF accessibility. |
| A harmless generation was rejected at the tool's output check; the production specialist later hit a usage limit. | Retry a simpler benign scene through the same tool; retain failed-attempt record. Coordinator completed saved layout with exact scope/verification; no paid API fallback or invented consultation. | Preserve partial artifacts, state actual limitations, continue authorized work; don't claim blocked-tool steps succeeded. |

The source of truth is the current plan, exact asset hashes, independent visual report, export manifest and verification.json. Formal visual acceptance is recorded in review gates by final hashes, while plan status marks selected-for-proof assets. No human child reading, printer proof, legal rights determination or accessibility certification was performed.


## Author rejection of composed edition

The author rejected the uniform rectangular pictures below the text as artificial; exact feedback is recorded in ../redesign/author-feedback.md. Earlier continuity/geometry checks and the visual pass did not establish successful page composition or author acceptance. They missed that scene scale and placement repeated regardless of emotional weight.

Correction: commission illustrations for actual text space and edge treatment; compare representative composed pages early; assess scale, transitions, negative space and sequence rhythm explicitly in final proof. Rectangles can be appropriate; this case does not establish a universal ban. New assets, previews and final proof have their own records under ../redesign/. Future work should run representative composition trials before commissioning the full set; this retrofit commissioned its replacements together, then corrected the actual page proofs.
