# Tavin — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Apprentice accountant.
**Age / build:** Goblin youth, nineteen, 4 ft 9, lanky and slightly stooped.
**Story function:** The apprentice who believes the people who sign the accounts read them, and who will not always accept Ragna’s caution.
**Canon anchors:** Six months an apprentice; fast at arithmetic; wants to distribute available money sooner; his mother Hedra cuts his hair badly; he is arrested later in the story.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Patchily cut black hair, uneven at the sides.
- Brown coat one size too large, sleeves slightly long.
- Pen behind the right ear.
- Ink stain on the left cuff, not the right.
- No beard, no mustache.
- Expression is quick and open, not brooding.

## Avoid

- A neat or fashionable haircut.
- A tailored coat, waistcoat or cravat.
- A confident swagger or hero pose.
- Modern glasses, headphones, or a backpack.
