# Wizarding World: project scope

An unofficial fan-fiction series set in the established Wizarding World, concentrating on goblin lives in eighteenth-century Britain. Published stories and official supplemental writing are external reference points, not project inventions. The project's characters, institutions, events and interpretations are provisional fiction; no claim of official status is made.

The Ministry of Magic exists by 1752. Goblins possess magic and practice metalwork; individual views on ownership vary. Avoid pretending that the surviving wizard-authored account is neutral, or that goblins form one political bloc. Do not claim the historical events of this series are established canon. See the first book's research notes.
