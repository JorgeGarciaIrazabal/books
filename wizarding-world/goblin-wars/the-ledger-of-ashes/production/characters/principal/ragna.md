# Ragna — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Protagonist. Accountant at the Works Bank counting house.
**Age / build:** Goblin woman, late forties, 4 ft 8, compact and upright.
**Story function:** The cautious keeper of other people’s records who discovers that protecting the books can protect an injustice.
**Canon anchors:** Knew Dorrik professionally for sixteen years; carries a desk key and a leather pouch; uses a penknife; ink-stained fingers; dark coat; values signed authority and fair procedure.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Key pouch on the right hip, penknife on the left.
- Ink stain on the middle finger of the right hand.
- Hair in a short working braid, never loose.
- Dark charcoal wool coat, never black leather.
- No spectacles, no wand, no jewelry.
- Expression is dry and controlled, not saintly or mournful.

## Avoid

- Hooked nose, greed imagery, comic goblin energy.
- Modern clothing, high heels, visible makeup.
- A saintly glow, martyr pose, or raised fist.
- A generic fantasy cloak or robes.
