# Nell and the Pocket-Sized Storm

## Reader and promise
An original, funny, warm fantasy for a parent to read aloud to a seven-year-old daughter. A capable girl finds a miniature talking storm in her boot and helps it catch up with its family. Playful dialogue, an impromptu garden band, a kite flight, and a cozy bedtime finish.
