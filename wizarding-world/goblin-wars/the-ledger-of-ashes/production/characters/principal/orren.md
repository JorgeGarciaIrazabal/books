# Orren — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Dorrik’s son. Rivet-sorter, boat-dreamer.
**Age / build:** Goblin boy, eight, 3 ft 6, thin and quick.
**Story function:** The child whose immediate needs keep the wage theft from becoming an abstraction. He carries the little wooden boat to the burial and takes it home again.
**Canon anchors:** He sorts rivets into a tray with ten compartments beneath the workbench; he wants materials for a boat; his coat is buttoned wrong; he carries a small wooden boat.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Coat buttoned wrong, every time.
- Small wooden boat in his hands or pocket.
- Boots too large for him.
- Hair sticking up, not neatly combed.
- Expression is serious and observing, not moppet-cute.

## Avoid

- Tearful orphan cliché, huge anime eyes, or a lollipop.
- Modern toys, sneakers, or a backpack.
- A clean, well-fitted coat.
- A smiling, carefree pose.
