# The Goblin Wars

Adult historical fantasy about work, property, family, and the making of political memory in the Wizarding World. The series looks outward from goblin homes and workplaces. Wizards have motives and choices of their own, but are not required to explain or rescue goblin characters. Violence has lasting costs; humour and ordinary loyalties survive it.

Provisional trilogy beginning in 1752. Each book resolves its central conflict while changing the conditions of the next. The official political timeline constrains broad outcomes; the local story, causes, alliances and people are original inventions.
