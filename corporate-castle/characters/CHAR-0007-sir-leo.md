# Sir Leo, Counsel of Liability

- ID: CHAR-0007
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `corporate-castle` canon rev 4 (`FACT-0003`, `FACT-0015`, `DEC-CC-0008`), 2026-11-26
- Story-time validity: Active in Liability Tower from Book 1 opening onward

## Role and agency
- **Role**: Counsel of Liability inside Liability Tower, reporting to Doctor Lois. A true **knight of Deep Magic** who demonstrates what Liability's branch of Deep Magic looks like when wielded properly.
- **Mastery (`FACT-0015`)**: Far more learned in the intricacies of Deep Magic's spells than Doctor Lois. Knows the technical loopholes and procedural safeguards deep inside the Spellbooks—and uses that mastery to protect the integrity of an investigation when politics try to smother it.
- **Want**: Clean, principled legal execution, dry humor to survive Corporate Castle, an easier workload thanks to a sharp partner, and recognition he rarely gets from Doctor Lois.
- **Personal foibles (the "friendship tax")**:
  - Feels mistreated and undervalued by Doctor Lois and vents about it to Marissa as a confidant—completely out of touch with the gulf between Lois's frostiness toward him inside Liability Tower and Lois's outright neglect of the unfunded ER Watch on a leaking barge.
  - Knows Marissa carries heavy operational and analytical weight even for him, and quietly takes advantage of how much easier her skill makes his job. For Marissa, tolerating both his out-of-touch venting and his quiet leaning on her labor is the unspoken tax she pays for his friendship and allyship.
- **Redeeming spine**: Despite those foibles, **he is there for Marissa when it counts**. He goes to bat for her when real stakes hit—even when it means absorbing Doctor Lois's fury directly.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Marissa (`CHAR-0001`)**: Genuine friend, ally, and confidant who treats Marissa as an equal licensed practitioner of Deep Magic and delights in her skill. Creates a nuanced, realistic undercurrent of irritation for Marissa (his out-of-touch complaints about Lois; the quiet tax of carrying weight for him), balanced by deep trust because he actually steps into the fire for her when it matters.
- **With Doctor Lois (`CHAR-0002`)**: Reports to her, quietly outclasses her in technical command of Deep Magic's spells, resents her coldness, and absorbs her wrath when he uses an outside-counsel technicality to go to bat for Marissa on the Bullying High Lord inquiry.

## Voice and dialogue
Dry wit, crisp understatement, gallows humor, and fluent coded legal language when sensitive politics cannot be said aloud.

Sample lines:
- (Dry wit / friendship tax): *"Your Ink-Bind just saved me three hours of translating Sales's delusions. Lois barely grunted at my memo this morning, by the way—truly brutal up here in the ivory tower."*
- (Coded language after taking the hit for Marissa): *"I pushed the technicality as far as the stone allows, Marissa. There is no more ground above this floor."*
