# The Three Paths — Creative Brief

## Working Promise
Book 1 of *Beyond the Waterfall*: an exciting, spooky-cozy illustrated read-together chapter book for seven-year-old Clara about eleven-year-old Clara crossing a hidden waterfall into **Glassvale Academy**, choosing the wild **Path of Pure Magic**, earning real trust with **Jorge** (Path of Makers) and **Marissa** (Path of Nature), and uniting all three Paths with her friends **Maya** and **Sam** to stop **Silas (The Gray Coat)** from stealing the **Crown Seal** of the **World-Glass**.

## Reader and Format
- **Audience:** Read together by a parent and seven-year-old Clara. Plain, vivid everyday English (~Grade 3–5 reading clarity) with crisp dialogue, concrete action, and Harry Potter / Narnia atmospheric magic.
- **Length:** 9 short chapters (~6,300 to 7,200 words total; ~700–800 words per chapter), long enough for rich bedtime chapters without dragging.
- **Illustrated Blended-Text Format:** 12 custom interior illustrations (generated in three 2x2 sheets) plus 1 dedicated portrait cover. Every interior illustration features a calm, softly painted negative-space zone so story text blends directly into the artwork on 7 x 9 in storybook PDF pages, alongside a clean reflowable EPUB 3 edition.

## Craft and Spoiler Constraints
- **Zero em dashes (`—`), zero en dashes (`–`), zero `--` stand-ins** in all manuscript narration and dialogue. Use standard double quotation marks (`"..."`), one speaker per paragraph.
- **No preachy morals or filler:** Let action, jokes, and consequences show the lessons.
- **Strict Spoiler Guard:** **Peter** must appear 100% warm, noble, protective, and trustworthy throughout Book 1. Never hint overtly at his Book 5 Hollow Circle allegiance; limit foreshadowing to his innocent antique brass sand-timer.
- **Relationship Arc:** Clara does not start out close to Jorge or Marissa; she earns real trust with both through shared work in Chapter 4 and Chapter 8.
