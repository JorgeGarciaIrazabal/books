# The Hot Potato of Corporate Castle — creative brief

## Working promise or thesis
A razor-sharp corporate-fantasy satire following **Marissa, Royal Inquisitor of the ER Watch**, as her thankless, underfunded team gets punted like a hot potato between two dysfunctionally self-serving spires, the **Tower of Vibes** and **Liability Tower**, while comedic cutaway scenes expose the panic, delusion, and buck-passing happening behind closed doors upstairs.

## Reader and format
- **Audience**: Adult readers who enjoy workplace satire, dry legal and HR realism translated into high-fantasy allegory, and deeply earned professional boundary payoffs.
- **Genre**: Satirical corporate fantasy.
- **Assumed knowledge**: Everyday familiarity with corporate tech/office life (Slack, Zoom, HR, Legal, PIPs, vesting cliffs); all legal and ER mechanics land cleanly through the castle lexicon without requiring a law degree.
- **Language**: English.
- **Intended edition**: Text-first digital reading edition (private/local until author authorizes publication).

## Premise / scope
When **High Lady Lexi of Vibe Operations** grows disgusted with upper court politics and vanishes overnight, her departure leaves a leadership vacuum in the **Tower of Vibes**, where the Courtiers of Optics are unequipped to manage a team speaking Deep Magic. Caught between a private **Offline Oath** to Lexi and pressure from **Lord Sterling**, **Doctor Lois, Grand Archivist of Liability**, is in no position to refuse. Sterling kicks the **ER Watch** across the moat into **Liability Tower** as an unfunded "temporary cross-tower experiment" with zero gold coin of budget, leaving **Marissa** and **Sarai** to find a cramped wooden barge moored outside Liability Tower's drawbridge and move the Watch aboard without Lois's help.

At first, **Marissa** and **Doctor Lois** work in crisp, effortless **Deep Magic** sync on ordinary risk, far preferable to the pastel circus across the moat. Then the fault line breaks open: Lois refuses to spend political capital fighting Sterling for gold, and when Marissa completes an airtight **Formal Inquiry** proving a **High Lord** is bullying their fiefdom, Lois folds to High Table politics and pressures Marissa to downgrade **Okta Excommunication** to hollow *coaching*. Respect dies on the spot. When **Sarai** returns from **Protected Exile** and resigns, Lois summons Marissa to break the news and tries to offload her back to **Baroness Orla** using botched **Illusion Magic** ("Interim Captain"). Marissa raises **The Mirror Shield**, refuses to jump on a call with Orla, and ends the call on Lois herself. Afterward, before speaking with Orla, Marissa sends a calm **severance scroll** request offering an orderly transition period as a peace offering, which leadership rejects, setting up the Cashmere Coup and the chess match to come.

## Aesthetic intention
- **Voice**: Dry, surgical, darkly hilarious, and unsentimental. The satire works because the institutional logic underneath the fantasy metaphors is painfully exact.
- **Comedic cutaways (author priority)**: Lean generously into behind-the-scenes comedic cutaway scenes across the twin towers (Doctor Lois snapping red fountain pens, Baroness Orla panicking under her ring light, Lord Sterling treating headcount like grain ledgers, Courtiers scrambling in Slack-runes) to sharpen dramatic irony against Marissa's calm stillness.
- **Magic divide (`DEC-CC-0001`)**: Strictly two schools (**Deep Magic** split between Spirit vs. Institutional Insulation, and **Illusion Magic**), plus Marissa's signature **Mirror Shield**. Zero **Cold Alchemy** references.
- **Restraint**: Let dialogue, stillness, and operational consequences carry the payoff rather than spelling out morals or over-explaining Marissa's free-will realization (per Tab 4's Chapter 1 checklist).
