# Illustration direction and handoff

## Scope and evidence

Twelve planned raster illustrations, two per five-page chapter, inside the existing 30-page reading edition. Exact manuscript snapshot hash, page locators, scene states and asset paths are in `illustration-plan.json`. All 30 original manuscript pages were read before the coordinator's six-file split. No images or finished layouts were inspected by this art-direction assignment. No cover commission or printer specification is implied.

## Sequence

Pages 2/4: intimate discovery and hospitality. Pages 6/9: handmade carriage and comic enlargement. Pages 12/15: private hurt and tentative participation. Pages 17/19: dog-level musical exchange and moving procession. Pages 23/24: careful preparation and expansive ascent. Pages 27/30: family reception and a small, still closing joke.

Each image depicts one moment. Do not combine before/after states in a montage. Place it after its page's prose so the page-30 rainbow remains the final beat. Generated lettering must not replace the manuscript's sound effects.

## Production geometry

Generate landscape 3:2 masters. Fit the entire image into the available maximum 5.9 × 2.5-inch area without distortion. At 2.5 inches high, a 3:2 image is 3.75 inches wide; it cannot fill the wider box without cropping. Center with paper breathing room. Keep faces, hands, kite suspension and crucial objects comfortably inside frame edges. Final layout inspection determines whether a page needs a smaller image. Preserve readable type and exactly 30 pages.

Record actual pixel dimensions and effective resolution at placement. No printer, stock, binding, bleed, color profile or minimum resolution has been commissioned here. Do not call this print-ready. Retain original masters alongside any derived files.

## Reusable sequence

1. Read current prose and disclosure boundaries; freeze page/scene locators and source hash.
2. Establish cast/object reference and palette; preserve actual reference paths and hashes.
3. Write each prompt from shared style plus one record's action, scale and exclusions.
4. Generate assets; record actual tool, prompt, references, date, dimensions and output hash. Keep status planned until an actual file exists.
5. Inspect every image for anatomy, continuity, readable composition, emotional tone and page-specific reveals. Repair material errors. Mark accepted only after review, retaining unresolved observations.
6. Update alt text to the actual accepted picture. Integrate and inspect all illustrated output pages at reading size, then verify page count and asset coverage.

The plan's alt texts are provisional scene descriptions. HTML needs meaningful alternatives for these narrative images. Confirm PDF tagging and reading order separately; HTML alt attributes alone do not prove an accessible PDF. Keep production credits/provenance outside story text. No third-party image licensing or human illustration commission was performed.

Next action: coordinator generates and inspects the twelve assets, updates provenance/status, and validates the actual exports. No canon changes proposed.
