# Nell and the Pocket-Sized Storm

## Reader and promise
An original, funny, warm fantasy for a parent to read aloud to a seven-year-old daughter. A capable girl finds a miniature talking storm in her boot and helps it catch up with its family. Playful dialogue, an impromptu garden band, a kite flight, and a cozy bedtime finish.

## Format and targets
Exactly 30 story pages, with the title included on story page 1, rather than extra cover or front matter. Aim for 1,800–2,400 words in English, roughly 50–95 words per page, with occasional shorter action pages. Provide an actual 30-page PDF and editable Markdown. Large text and light vector ornaments; a fully illustrated edition was not requested.

## Boundaries
No frightening peril, violence, villains, humiliation presented as deserved, or explicit lesson paragraph. Let the girl make decisions and repair a small social mistake. Keep the older caregiver present. Preserve humor and the cloud's agency. Magical pocket-storm behavior is fictional, not a factual account of meteorology. No existing-book dependence.

## Assumptions
The request was in English, so the book is in English. Name, character identities, fantasy premise, and visual styling are reversible creative choices delegated by the open request. The daughter's name and preferences were not supplied. No claim is made that the protagonist represents her.

The author retains taste, rights, and publication decisions. This is a private reading deliverable, with no publication, upload, or invented author credit.
