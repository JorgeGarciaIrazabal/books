# Maya

- ID: CHAR-0006
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glasshaven` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GH-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 onward

## Role and agency
Maya (11) is Clara's calm, big-hearted friend on the Path of Roots. Quiet around crowds, she never backs down when a living creature or a friend is trapped. She brings patience, weather sense, and teamwork to Clara's trio.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0001`)**: Grounds Clara when Aether magic gets dizzy or cold.
- **With Pebble**: Caretaker of **Pebble**, a shy baby Mossback turtle with three glowing clover-ferns on its mossy shell.

## Voice and dialogue
Gentle, observant, unexpectedly funny when dryly pointing out Leo's explosions.

Sample line:
"The wind just went flat and cold, Clara. Even the gulls stopped talking. Something inside that tower is awake."

## Physical and historical facts
- Pronouns: she/her. Age: 11.
- Appearance: Warm brown skin, thick black hair in two neat braids tucked into a moss-green hooded cloak, fingerless knitted green mittens, satchel of Seed-Stones, cradling her baby Mossback turtle Pebble.
