# The World of Glasshaven

## Realm overview
Glasshaven is a hidden magical realm tucked behind the shimmering **Mistwall** along a rugged northern sea. Ordinary people see only empty grey water and sea fog. Invited eleven-year-old students cross through the **Fog Arch**, a stone doorway on Platform Zero of an old seaside train station, or through hidden brass-hinged doors along the coast.

Three major landmarks anchor the realm:
- **Lanternhold Academy**: A cliffside island school built of pale sea-stone, copper domes, hanging gardens, and starlit towers.
- **Cinderport**: A cozy lantern-lit harbor town on the mainland edge, connected to Lanternhold by ferry and wind-skiff.
- **The Whispering Pines and the Sunken Bells**: An ancient silver-barked forest that speaks in wind-chimes, and half-flooded coastal ruins that hum softly with old Pure Magic when the tide turns.

## Lanternhold Academy & First-Term Foundations
All eleven-year-old first-year students live at Lanternhold Academy and begin in **First-Term Foundations**, learning the basics of every discipline together before choosing their specialization at the **Mid-Term Crossing** by ringing the **Choosing Bell**. Even after choosing a Path, students share meals, courtyard games, and friendships across all three Paths.

Lanternhold has four connected zones:
1. **The Brass Terraces** (Path of Spark): Steep stone steps, steam chimneys, telescope balconies, testing yards, and copper-domed workshops.
2. **The Living Cloister** (Path of Roots): Warm glasshouses, rain-fed pools, mossy archways, beast-care stables, and living oak bridges.
3. **The Star Spire** (Path of Stars): A tall midnight-blue stone tower crowned by an open star-observatory. Far beneath its deepest staircase lies the sealed **Heart-Vault** (and above its upper landing hangs the cracked **Whispering Bell**).
4. **The Crossroads Court**: The central stone yard where all three zones meet around the **Threefold Lantern**, whose flame burns gold, green, and silver together.

## The Three Paths

### 1. Path of Spark (Engineering & Science)
- **Leader Teacher**: Master Jorge
- **Colors & Emblem**: Copper orange and cobalt blue; a brass compass nested inside a gear.
- **Power Source & Mechanics**: Channels **Glimmer**, a measurable energy stored in amber spark-cells, springs, lenses, and copper coils. Every device must be drafted, built, tested, and repaired through trial and error.
- **Strengths & Limits**: Most reliable Path. Once calibrated, a Spark tool works almost every single time without draining the user's feelings. Its limit is time, parts, and physics: a rush job snaps or misfires.
- **Typical Personality Traits**: Curious, stubborn tinkerers, practical planners, calm under pressure, happiest with goggles on and chalk on their hands.
- **Milder Dark Side (Rustwork)**: Using unsafe shortcuts, spy-locks, or warmth-draining machines that steal power or choice from others. Harmful and messy, though almost always repairable by dismantling and rebuilding fairly.

### 2. Path of Roots (Nature & Living World)
- **Leader Teacher**: Master Marissa
- **Colors & Emblem**: Moss green and golden amber; a braided oak-leaf ring.
- **Power Source & Mechanics**: Partners with **Breath**, the shared pulse connecting weather, soil, plants, and animals. Root-walkers never force nature; they listen first and ask through rhythm, breath, and teamwork.
- **Strengths & Limits**: Most balanced and spiritual Path. Ideal for healing, shelter, navigation, calming creatures, and reading storms. Its limit is consent and harmony: nature refuses anger, selfishness, or panic.
- **Typical Personality Traits**: Patient listeners, steady team-builders, observant, fiercely protective of living things.
- **Milder Dark Side (Thornwork / Blight)**: Twisting vines, choking fog, or frightened beasts out of fear or greed. Hurts both the land and the caster, yet heals when balance and trust are restored.

### 3. Path of Stars (Pure Magic)
- **Leader Teacher**: Master Peter
- **Colors & Emblem**: Midnight indigo and silver-gold; an open silver crescent cradling a falling star.
- **Power Source & Mechanics**: Draws directly on **Aether**, raw starlight-magic shaped by clear imagination, fierce willpower, and a focus object (most often a silver **Star-Pin**).
- **Strengths & Limits**: By far the strongest Path, able to conjure shields, blinding light, levitation, distant voices, and shimmering illusions. Yet Aether is wild, full of ancient mysteries, and terribly hard to steer. A distracted or angry spell can shatter or backfire.
- **Typical Personality Traits**: Bold dreamers, brave, intense, deeply loyal, with bright imaginations and quick tempers.
- **Major Dark Side (The Hollow / Shadow Magic)**: Cold ink-black smoke rimmed in pale violet, fed by envy, terror, or stolen power. Hollow Magic devours warmth, light, sound, and memories, leaves frost-grey scars, and whispers sweet shortcuts to pull Star-casters deeper. Its ancient source is the **Quiet Crown**, sealed long ago behind the **Threefold Keys**.

## Magical Creatures
- **Emberfoxes**: Cat-sized cinnamon foxes with warm glowing lantern-tips on their tails; shy around strangers, playful around honest hearts.
- **Cloud-Owls**: Silvery mist-feathered owls that carry academy letters and ruffle their feathers when dark magic or storms draw near.
- **Mossbacks**: Friendly dog-sized turtles carrying tiny glowing ferns and pebbles on their shells.
- **Shadow-Moths**: Spooky pale-black moths with silver eye-spots that flutter where Hollow Magic leaks, swallowing all sound for ten seconds when startled.
- **Stone Hounds**: Carved guardian statues perched along the Star Spire; still for centuries unless woken by dark magic.

## Key Magical Objects
- **The Whispering Bell**: A cracked silver-iron tower bell above the Star Spire that cannot be heard by ordinary ears; when Hollow Magic touches the island, it rings silently inside Star-students' heads.
- **Star-Pins**: Silver pins worn by Path of Stars apprentices to steady Aether; they glow warm silver when calm and flicker violet when Hollow Magic nears.
- **Spark-Compasses**: Brass pocket tools built by Spark apprentices to measure Glimmer leaks and point toward true magnetic and magical north.
- **Seed-Stones**: Warm river pebbles sung to by Root apprentices; they sprout temporary rope-vines, moss cushions, or lantern-blooms in emergencies.
- **The Threefold Keys & Heart-Vault**: Three ancient locks (Brass, Living Wood, and Star-Glass) sealing the **Heart-Vault** beneath Lanternhold.
