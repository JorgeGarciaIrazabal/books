# Book scope
Read ancestor AGENTS.md files, brief.md, book.json, and the assigned review or
creation target. Creators and the coordinator also read handoff.md and relevant
outline/style/canon records. For a fresh manuscript review, withhold handoff,
planning outline, drafter rationale, and prior verdicts until the first assessment.
For an explicitly assigned outline review, read the outline as the target and state
that manuscript execution remains unassessed. book.json owns chapter order.
Drafts and outlines do not accept canon. Submit proposed changes separately.
Record state and disclosures at scene/section boundaries. Independent reviewers
receive actual text and scope without the drafter's defense.
Return findings and coverage to the coordinator; the coordinator updates handoff.md
and shared ledgers after meaningful work. Preserve human prose.
