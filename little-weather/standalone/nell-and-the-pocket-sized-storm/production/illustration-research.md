# Illustration practice: inspected sources

Accessed 2026-10-25. These are primary publisher, professional-association and standards-body pages. Practice below is paraphrased; edition applications are this assignment's judgments. No market research, workshop attendance or full-course review is claimed.

## R-ART-001: Penguin, How to become a book illustrator

URL: https://www.penguin.co.uk/about/company-articles/how-to-become-a-book-illustrator

Inspected sections: “How does an art department find an illustrator they want to work with?”, “Create your portfolio around your original style”, and “How much freedom do you get as the illustrator?” The publisher interviews Puffin art director Ben Hughes and illustrator Nadia Shireen. They discuss matching style to a book, original visual identity, active characters and art-department/illustrator collaboration.

Application: coherent cast and palette, with action-led scenes rather than repeated posed portraits. Broad media characteristics establish an original direction. Limit: this article describes professional practice; it neither endorses generated art nor validates this edition's quality.

## R-ART-002: SCBWI, Picture Book Physicality, Miranda Paul

URL: https://www.scbwi.org/events/scbwi-virtual-winter-conference-2025/picture-book-physicality-turning-an-idea-or-short-story-into-a-32-40-page-experience-with-agent-miranda-paul

Inspected public description, schedule and objectives. The listed teaching topics include storyboarding, page turns, illustration variety, angle and pacing across a book.

Application: assess the twelve pictures as a sequence, vary viewpoint and reserve reveals for their assigned pages. Limit: only the public course listing was read, not the recorded workshop. Its 32/40-page examples are not universal requirements and do not override this requested 30-page reading edition.

## R-ART-003: W3C WAI, Informative Images

URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/informative/

Inspected overview and examples 2 and 4. Alternatives should communicate the image's contextual meaning; informative versus decorative treatment depends on purpose.

Application: give each narrative illustration concise contextual alt text and revise it after inspecting actual output. Limit: web-image guidance does not certify PDF accessibility or physical-print quality.
