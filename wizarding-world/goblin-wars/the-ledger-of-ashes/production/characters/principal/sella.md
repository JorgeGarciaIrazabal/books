# Sella — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Dorrik’s widow. Maker, bookkeeper and the first name on the workshop plate.
**Age / build:** Goblin woman, forties, 4 ft 7, solid and upright.
**Story function:** The widow who needs money now, refuses an ambiguous release, and forces the investigation to answer to the households behind the figures.
**Canon anchors:** Her name comes first on the brass doorplate; she is an accurate bookkeeper with small, upright handwriting; she negotiates credit; she holds the original fragment and the body receipt; paying her workers is separate from the investigation.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Hair bound back tightly, with a few grey threads.
- Pale stone dust on the right shoulder.
- Dark work dress under a leather apron.
- Sleeves rolled to the elbow.
- No veil, no mourning gloves, no jewelry.
- Expression is steady and contained, not theatrical grief.

## Avoid

- A weeping widow cliché or fainting pose.
- Glamorous makeup, loose hair, or a black veil.
- A modern apron or machine-made clothing.
- A saintly glow or a passive stance.
