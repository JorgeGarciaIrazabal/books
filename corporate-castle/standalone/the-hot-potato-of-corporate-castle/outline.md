## Throughline (Front Half — Active)

1. **The Punt (Prologue — LOCKED & CLEANED)**: High Lady Lexi grows disgusted with upper court politics, extracts an Offline Oath from Doctor Lois to shelter the ER Watch, says goodbye to Marissa and Sarai, and vanishes, leaving a leadership vacuum in the Tower of Vibes. Caught between her Offline Oath and pressure from Lord Sterling, Lois cannot refuse; Sterling rebrands the punt as a "temporary cross-tower experiment" and kicks the ER Watch across the moat into Liability Tower with zero gold coin of budget, leaving Marissa and Sarai to find a cramped wooden barge moored outside Liability Tower's drawbridge and move the Watch aboard without Lois's help.
2. **The Reluctant Steward & Deep Magic Sync (Chapter 1 — DRAFTED)**: Real-time cold open: Marissa dismantles **Elliot, High Lord of Engineering**, in a 15-minute Deep Magic vise (from cooperative off-ramp to razor-sharp iron fist after his smug calendar-invite stall, protecting witness confidentiality while trapping his *"preoccupied"* / *"I don't recall"* deflections until he folds in quiet fear and shame), followed immediately by Liability Tower's icy loudspeaker welcome while Lois calmly pulls the velvet curtain shut on the barge. Cutaway to Sienna's two-hour ego-repair therapy session with Elliot; clean gold-standard file close with Lois, Sir Leo, and a stunned Baroness Orla (establishing what Deep Magic looks like when it works as meant); Leo's *"Like a knife through butter"* Slack-rune and out-of-touch venting (friendship tax); Lois's *"packed by the door"* cutaway; Victoria and Riley intuitively practicing Deep Magic with Warlords on the barge; Sarai guarding the door; and the quiet *"only three weeks"* Protected Exile hook.
3. **The Unfunded Trap & The Fracture (Chapter 2 — READY TO DRAFT)**: Simmering unfunded-barge friction builds as Sarai's three-week Protected Exile stretches toward three months and Doctor Lois refuses to spend political capital fighting Sterling for gold coin. Early Mirror Shield stillness surfaces in Marissa. Meanwhile, **Sir Leo** brings dry wit, deep respect for Marissa's craft, and genuine allyship—alongside a quiet undercurrent of irritation for Marissa as he vents out-of-touch complaints about Lois's frostiness from inside Liability Tower and quietly lets Marissa carry heavy weight even for him (the unspoken tax of his friendship). Then comes **The Fracture**: Marissa builds an airtight Formal Inquiry proving a High Lord is bullying their fiefdom and brings her pushback to **Leo first**. Proving himself a true knight of Deep Magic (far more learned in spell intricacies than Lois), Leo goes to bat for Marissa—securing a **Silk-Robed Sellsword of Outside Counsel** to interview the High Lord on a technicality—and takes it up to **Doctor Lois**. In a blistering comedic-dramatic cutaway behind closed doors, Lois explodes in fury that Leo stood up for Marissa and escalated a High Lord matter. Leo absorbs the hit directly, then returns to Marissa speaking in **coded language** to tell her he has taken it as far as he was able. Marissa must swallow the bitter pill of agreeing to hollow *coaching* in the end—shattering every last ounce of respect she had for Lois and exposing Lois as a coward and a hidden High Lord of Vibes hiding behind her glowing red pen.
4. **The Mirror Shield, the Peace Offering, and the Cashmere Coup (Chapter 3 — READY TO DRAFT/REWRITE)**:
   - **Beat A — Sarai's Resignation**: Sarai returns from Protected Exile and tells Marissa she is resigning.
   - **Beat B — The 1:1 Showdown with Doctor Lois (Tab 4)**: Doctor Lois summons Marissa into a Zoom lair to break the news and ship the Watch back to the Tower of Vibes. Lois casts a nervous fishing line (*"Maybe you already knew. It's okay if you did."*). Marissa raises **The Mirror Shield** through stony, unblinking silence. Rattled, Lois deploys botched Illusion Magic, claiming it was a mistake to leave the Watch siloed and calling Marissa **"Interim Captain"** for the first time. Marissa asks one clean operational question: whether Orla is backfilling Sarai. When Lois admits she does not know, Marissa states the unspinnable truth (everything will keep falling on her), refuses Lois's desperate attempt to punt her onto an immediate call with Orla, and ends the call on Lois herself.
   - **Beat C — The Peace Offering (`DEC-HPCC-0002`)**: After hanging up on Lois and **before** speaking with Orla, Marissa sends a calm, professional E-parchment requesting a **severance scroll** in exchange for an orderly transition period: a clean peace offering that gives Corporate Castle a dignified off-ramp.
   - **Beat D — Comedic Cutaway: The Cashmere Coup**: Panic erupts across both spires. In Lord Sterling's stark war chamber, Sterling flatly refuses to pay gold coin for a transition, Lois drafts the sanitized rejection, and Baroness Orla seizes the moment to pull the ER Watch back into Vibes for zero incremental gold coin by quietly planning to rebrand Sarai's Captain seat into **Chief Steward of ER** and layer Marissa underneath.
   - **Beat E — Orla's Call & The Aftermath**: Marissa receives the rejection and takes her call with Orla, seeing straight through the glossy spin. Orla claims the ER Squires, cancels Riley's one-on-ones, and pressures Riley to rubber-stamp an undocumented ADA termination.
5. **The Silent Chess Match & The Brick Wall (Chapter 4 — ALIGNED WITH TAB 2 CH 3)**: Orla runs secret interviews for Ziva while freezing Marissa off the panels, drops the Chief Steward layering news with ninety seconds left in a 1:1, and gets hit with Marissa's **Ancient Precedent** severance E-parchment. Comedic cutaways capture Orla's panic, Sterling's spreadsheet indifference, Lois turning Orla into a "velvet human shield" over Sir Leo's candid warning, and Orla's meltdown after Marissa shatters her Buzzword Hypnosis in Monday's 1:1 and ignores her panicked offer of Lois's org chart. Lois issues her smug *"do not poke the bear — they always stay"* decree.
