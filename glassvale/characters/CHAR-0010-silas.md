# Silas, "The Gray Coat" (Book 1 Antagonist)

- ID: CHAR-0010
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1

## Visual Identity
Tall, gaunt intruder in a rain-dark slate-gray coat with a high storm collar and silver-etched mask, eyes shadowed beneath a dark hood, flanked by Whisper-Moths and smoky emerald-eyed Ink-Hounds.

## Role and Agency
Field agent for **The Hollow Circle** sent to steal the **Crown Seal** from Glassvale's Bell-Vault. Uses Cold-Iron tools, Blight pins, and Shadow Magic creatures to break academy defenses, believing Pure Magic deserves to rule alone. Foiled by the three Paths ringing together in Chapter 8; flees into mist without revealing who unlocked the outer academy gate for him.

## Voice and Dialogue
Quiet, icy, measured, unhurried.

Sample line:
"A bell can only ring if someone dares to crack the glass, child."
