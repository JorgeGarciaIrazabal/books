# Chapter 8: The Song of the Three Bells

"Get clear of the stairs!" Jorge shouted, blasting the frost off the outer doorway as he stormed into the ground hall with a roaring brass flash-drum strapped over his shoulder.

Right behind him swept Marissa, her emerald cloak whipping dry in the draft while Pip swooped overhead and thick living ivy vines raced like green snakes across the cold flagstones.

"Silas is still inside the vault!" Clara cried, pointing down the open trapdoor as Maya and Sam leaped off the bottom steps.

All five charged back down into the circular Bell-Vault.

Inside, Silas stood inches from the shimmering pedestal. The Pure Magic cage was cracked halfway down the middle, trembling under a swirling spear of dark emerald-violet Shadow Magic. Behind him, both Ink-Hounds crouched low, snarling silently.

"Stay back!" Silas snapped, sweeping one gloved hand sideways.

A wall of biting black frost slammed across the floor, freezing Jorge's brass boots to the tiles and turning Marissa's rushing vines brittle as winter glass. Even Jorge's flash-drum sputtered low under the crushing shadow chill.

Silas turned back toward the trembling golden Crown Seal, raising both hands for the final blow.

"Wait!" Sam yelled, flipping frantically to the very back page of his rain-spotted Star-Notebook, where he had copied the old Bell-Tower carving from Common Term. "Look at the three bells! Walls and locks are not the room's true guard. The founders protected it with a song! Brass, Leaf, and Star together!"

Clara looked at Jorge straining against the ice on his flash-drum, then at Marissa coaxing warmth back into her frozen ivy beside Pip. Pure Magic could not break Silas's dark shield alone. Makers could not reach the pedestal past the frost. Nature could not cut through Shadow Magic without starlight.

"Jorge! Aim your copper coil at Marissa's vines!" Clara shouted, dropping into a steady stance in the middle of the vault. "Maya, Sam, sprint up to the bell ropes!"

Jorge did not waste a second doubting her. He cranked his flash-drum lever hard, sending a thick golden beam of steady Maker spark across the frosty floor straight into Marissa's outstretched palm.

Marissa smiled fiercely, let the warm spark run through living green sap, and flung three bright, sunlit ivy stems whipping forward across the vault toward Clara's lifted hands.

Clara caught the living vines without flinching. Instead of pushing raw magic blindly outward, she remembered every lesson from all three Paths: Jorge's calm measurement, Marissa's quiet listening, and Peter's steady shape. She wove her bright violet-gold Pure Magic spark cleanly *through* the copper-lit vines, turning them into a glowing three-colored braid of gold, emerald, and starlight.

"Now!" Clara shouted.

Just above the open trapdoor in the ground hall, Maya hauled the thick vine rope of the **Leaf Bell**. Sam threw all his weight onto the silver cord of the **Star Bell**. And across the flagstones, Nora and Ben burst in from the courtyard and yanked the **Brass Bell** together with all their might!

*BONG! CHIME! RING!*

Three mighty notes crashed down through the stone vault as one single, shaking chord.

Bright three-colored light erupted from Clara's braided beam, sweeping across the entire room like sunrise breaking through fog. Both smoky Ink-Hounds shattered into harmless mist. The black frost melted off Jorge's boots in one hiss of warm steam.

Headmistress Monica herself hurried down the vault stairs in answer to Sam's signal flare, holding an iron ward-box open just as the golden Crown Seal snapped smoothly inside and the lid slammed shut.

"This is not finished, Headmistress!" Silas hissed, backing fast into a swirling pocket of cold green smoke. "Glassvale holds four more pieces of the World-Glass, and **The Hollow Circle** never forgets a lock!"

With one sharp snap of his cloak, Silas vanished into empty air, leaving only faint gray frost fading slowly off the flagstones.

Seconds later, rain-soaked footsteps pounded down the stairs behind Monica. Peter arrived breathless in drenched plum robes, his bright gold shield already raised. Seeing the trio safe and the ward-box locked, he lowered his arm and let out a huge, relieved breath. "Monica! Something tripped the Mistgate outer alarm in the storm, and by the time I cleared the rocks, I feared I was too late. Are you all unharmed?"

Silence settled over the vault for one heartbeat. Then Pebble let out a loud, triumphant *PEEP!* and everybody cheered until the ancient bells overhead hummed softly back.
