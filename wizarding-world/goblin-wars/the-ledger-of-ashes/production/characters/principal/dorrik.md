# Dorrik — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Fitter and delivery worker. The case’s human centre, deceased before the story begins.
**Age / build:** Goblin man, forties, 4 ft 8, broad hands, strong forearms.
**Story function:** The dead worker whose missing wages and disputed fall turn an audit into an investigation. He is remembered through his family, his tools and his badly mended cup.
**Canon anchors:** A skilled gate-fitter; poor at mending his own cup; one polished boot and one split sole; a small blue cup with a string-wrapped handle; a household of fitters and hinge-makers.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon. Use the living portrait for memory, portrait-on-the-wall, or flashback. Use the world sheet for the trailer’s death imagery.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Split sole on the left boot; right boot polished.
- Small blue cup with a string-wrapped handle.
- Short black beard and close-cropped hair.
- Broad hands, visible calluses.
- Leather apron over a work coat.
- He is warm and specific, not a saint or a ghost.

## Avoid

- Corpse horror, blood, or a visible body.
- A ghostly glow, halo, or angelic pose.
- A red waistcoat, formal suit, or gentleman’s clothes.
- A neat, unused cup.
