# Current story state

After SC-0002: Harrow has the ledger; Sella holds the original torn fragment, and Ragna and Neri have copies. Ragna, Tavin and Ilva saw the concealed page to differing degrees; Sella and Neri know Merrok signed it but not why. Dorrik is dead; the circumstances are unknown. His family received twenty bars against a signed note for forty; a duplicate charge also appears in the ledger. The workshop has made temporary individual wage arrangements. Neri will accompany Ragna to Blackwater on a latch commission; Sella intends to speak to Brann.

After SC-0003: Ragna and Neri saw two release entries of twenty bars; a strike and mark are unexplained. Brann saw Dorrik dead at the bottom and says the lift came down wrong; he points to a dismantled rail without stating when or why. Pell took Ragna’s notes and restricted removals. Neri and Ragna have reconstructed a drawing. Tavin remains at the counting house.

After SC-0004: Merrok has offered conditional relief but retained the money; households will discuss it at the old weigh house at two after Dorrik’s burial. Two partner signatures can authorise reserve payments, but Merrok says two partners refused without the underlying ledger. Brann and his sister have moved belongings into Tavin’s mother’s home by agreement. Ragna and Tavin are proposing an independently governed mutual fund.

After SC-0005: Dorrik is buried. Four households, including Brelk, accepted Merrok’s conditional payment. A separate two-day food and fuel fund is held by Sella, Fenn and Vessa, with Ragna keeping receipts. Seven households obtained arrangements. Two petitions seek bank wage verification and Ministry records and a hearing; Brann signed only observed facts. Tavin and Ragna disagree about how broadly names should be copied.

After SC-0006: The petitions are lodged. Ilva admits directing concealment of a reconciliation and agrees to explain more tomorrow at the workshop. Daniel Venn admits changing the second twenty-bar destination to Thorn depot on written instruction routed through Harrow’s office; he retains the dispatch duplicate, and Sella and Ragna hold attested copies. A separate forty-bar procurement reference exists, but Venn has no payment voucher. His mother must choose whether to move; no protection or immunity has been promised.

After SC-0007: Ilva admits certifying a separate forty-bar advance from pooled reserve, checking its transfer to the iron ledger, and concealing the reconciliation. Her signed control-book copies and advance authority link the purchase to Venn’s reference, but payment and receiving vouchers remain unverified. Issa and Halrik are the reserve partners approached; Ragna has signed a request for verified net wages. Sella receives discounted early payment for school hinges, covering Hirk and part of the workers’ wages. Edda took the conditional offer but her aunt was omitted from the register; she requests coalition aid.

After SC-0008: Venn’s mother has chosen to move to daughter Ruth. Venn retains the original dispatch and permits the renewed request to use facts and his name as witness, but not a named public wall display. Harrow refused an independent hearing; Issa will examine reconstructed balances with Halrik. Merrok suspended Ragna from certification for review with three days’ wages. Arven holds original bank slips and Ragna’s desk key and certified the working copies. Neri has dictated her separate account of the rail.

After SC-0009: The community agreed an anonymous narrow abstract and to delay copies until after the bank meeting. Tavin posted six early without changing the agreed words or exposing private names. Venn has lodged a signed statement, retains the original dispatch and consents to the use of its reference but not public posting of his name. Harrow, Pell and two other wizards have arrived at the weigh house and demand Venn accompany them; Tavin is at the latch. No violence has yet occurred.

After SC-0010: Venn surrendered the original dispatch but was detained and disarmed; Tavin was bound by Pell after helping Fenn. Harrow said they were being taken to Blackwater, for the time being, and holds the dispatch in his black case. Fenn’s wrist is injured; Edda’s cheek is cut and lantern broken; another bystander has a chest injury. Reed is cut and a healer attends. Ragna has a minor jaw cut and goes to tell Tavin’s mother; Neri holds the remaining papers with Sella witnessing.

After SC-0011: Tavin’s mother Hedra and Ragna obtain Pell’s written confirmation that both Tavin and Venn are held at Blackwater, and acceptance of Tavin’s clothing; no visit or letter allowed. Vessa and Brann remain in Hedra’s home; Brann does not consent to further circulation of his statement. Neri consents to use of her dated rail account. Dask, the injured bystander, is home; his recollection of a thrown stool is uncertain and private. The fund cannot cover care or broken spectacles. Partners are asked to examine balances at Sella’s workshop.

After SC-0012: Issa and Halrik paid verified net balances from the Works Bank partners’ working reserve to Tesh, Fenn and Edda, crediting Edda’s earlier bank relief but not gift aid; disputed claims were preserved. Sella’s iron balance remains unresolved. Issa holds Ilva’s original control book under receipt; partners agreed the Ministry advance requires separate accounting. Merrok offered reinstatement and a narrowed settlement, which Ragna did not accept. Hedra and Vessa returned to Blackwater; Ragna and Neri will seek the supplier’s copy.

After SC-0013: Mere Foundry’s supplier Joan Mere and stock supervisor Hask attest a separate forty-bar invoice, Works Bank payment-order settlement and Blackwater foreman Kelm’s acceptance. Ragna holds copies; the supplier keeps originals. The distinct batch marks can be imitated and do not prove later destination. Mere authorised Ragna and Neri to collect and inspect her damaged cart at Thorn tomorrow, but not to pledge the foundry or remove Ministry material. Her driver Borr is away downriver and has not yet chosen whether to testify. Venn’s aunt is Marian Lorne. A Derbyshire road checkpoint checks stores; a guard refers vaguely to trouble in the north.

After SC-0014: Ragna and Neri are in Thorn outer stores under authority to collect Mere’s damaged cart. A return book says the later forty-bar cargo was received complete, but they cannot inspect the depot receipt or identify the batch from a distance. Kelm, the Blackwater loading foreman, is held under the pretext of assisting the inquiry; his pass and departure chit are withheld. He says Harrow presses him on the rail, has a carrier’s counterfoil for the later forty in his jacket, knows a separate twenty entry exists but not who signed it, and explicitly wants to leave. Both exits are guarded.

After SC-0015: Kelm gave Ragna the carrier’s original counterfoil documenting receipt of the later forty bars, Dole’s signature and batch. He admitted to Neri he removed the rail and said Dorrik objected and Harrow ordered work to continue; a complete account and independent corroboration remain outstanding. They tried to leave Thorn in Mere’s cart; a guard struck its wheel, injuring Neri’s ankle. They abandoned the cart and Neri’s tools, reached the ruined kiln with the help of a charcoal carrier, and Kelm went voluntarily for a healer, carrier and Sella. Ragna retains the counterfoil; Tavin and Venn remain held.

After SC-0016: Kelm returned with a healer, carrier and Sella. Neri is hospitalised overnight with a crush injury. Mere will claim the cart hire cost; her original forty-bar counterfoil remains with Sella by agreement. Kelm signed a detailed account, witnessed by Sella and Mere as to signature rather than events: he removed the rail, Dorrik objected, Harrow told him to continue, the load shifted and Dorrik disappeared over the edge; Brann called from below. He says he refused to sign Pell’s false account. Neri asked to hear the rest in Sella’s presence. Kelm will stay at Mere’s foundry.

After SC-0018: Harrow offered to release Tavin and Venn if the forty-bar counterfoil is returned and Ilva withdraws her statement; Tavin rejects a bargain made for him. Borr signed a limited account: he transported the earlier twenty to Thorn and counted with clerk Sorn, but did not see the receiving-book entry. Rell will not yet show her load number. Issa will discuss setting the first twenty aside as disputed and paying Sella with Halrik. The Ministry has ordered the remaining reserve records surrendered tomorrow at noon.

After SC-0022: Tavin and Venn are free pending inquiry; Vale removed Harrow from directing the transfer inquiry without determining guilt. Issa and Halrik removed Merrok’s signing authority pending examination, authorised corrected wage payments, credited conditional relief and reserved open claims. The iron ledger largely burned; fragments, copies and control records survive, reserve files remain sealed under order, and some private balances are disputed. Sella’s household received its verified money and circulated a factual notice of Dorrik’s death. Neri is recovering, Fenn’s wrist remains injured, Ilva has left the bank, and Ragna’s bank job ended. Tavin and Ragna publish a limited account with witness consent. Wider unrest continues.
