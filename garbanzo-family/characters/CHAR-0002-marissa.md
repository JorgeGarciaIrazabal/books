# Marissa (Mom & Keeper of Real Life)

- ID: CHAR-0002
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `garbanzo-family` canon rev 4 (`DEC-GF-0006`, `DEC-GF-0008`), 2026-11-23
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Role and agency
Marissa is the warm, sharp-eyed heart of the family. Thirty-eight at the start of the quest, she makes sure big dreams stay livable for real humans: fair 50/50 footing between partners, a real cash cushion for stormy years, and a Spanish neighborhood full of neighbors, bakeries, cafés, parks, and schools rather than empty tourist streets or endless house repairs.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0001`)**: Spots wishful math in two seconds flat and pulls out the yellow legal pad, yet keeps hot coffee ready when Manhattan trains leave him wiped out.
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0003`)**: Co-conspirator in morning pancakes, kitchen dancing, and keeping big moves joyful for a six-year-old.
- **On fairness**: Will not settle for vague promises; insists every big luxury shows how many extra winter commutes it costs (`FACT-0003`).

## Voice and dialogue
Clear, playful, warm, and crisp when puncturing a bad pitch.

Sample line:
"Pretty pine trees, Jorge. Now point to the bakery, the school bus, and one neighbor who isn't a squirrel."

## Stable facts and contested beliefs
- **Accepted fact**: Loves warm family meals like Aunt Lidia's *cocido madrileño* and sunny, lived-in neighborhoods near shops like RÍO (`FACT-0006`, `FACT-0014`).
- **Name note**: Author-confirmed canonical spelling **Marissa** (`DEC-GF-0006`).
