# Clara (The Six-Year-Old Oracle & Girl-Wizard)

- ID: CHAR-0003
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `garbanzo-family` canon rev 1, 2026-11-22
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Role and agency
Clara is six years old, quick-witted, fearless, and the emotional engine of the quest. Far from being passive cargo in an adult relocation plot, she audits the plan with fierce honesty: checking for real adventures, dance floors, ice rinks, ponies, bikes, readable books with bold adventures (never dreary baby primers), and whether their cat Poki will still find them across an ocean.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0001`) & Marissa (`CHAR-0002`)**: Watches both parents more closely than they realize. Speaks English with effortless bounce and picks up Spanish phrases like stray glitter, correcting grown-ups whenever they sound scared.
- **With Poki (`CHAR-0005`)**: Sees Poki's starlight trail more clearly than either adult and refuses to leave behind the memory of their sleek companion.
- **With Garbanzo (`CHAR-0004`)**: Loves when Garbanzo tosses confetti and insists on naming every glowing crystal.

## Voice and dialogue
Bright, theatrical, unnervingly perceptive, and allergic to boring explanations.

Sample line:
"If the dragon steals the mornings, Papa, why do we keep giving him Tuesday?"

## Stable facts and contested beliefs
- **Accepted fact**: Six years old (`FACT-0008`); loves unicorns, dragons, girl-wizards, dancing, ice skating, horse riding, biking, and painting.
