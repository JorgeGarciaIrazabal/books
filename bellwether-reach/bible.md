# Bellwether Reach
An original secondary world of tidal cities governed by reciprocal flood compacts. Technology is mechanical: pumps, gates, paper records, hand bells. No electricity, prophecy, mind reading, or truth-detecting magic. Geography and flood rights create political choices; competence has human costs.
This is an authorized synthetic benchmark setting, not a claim about real hydraulic engineering. The fixed facts below constrain all candidates; new inventions remain proposals.
