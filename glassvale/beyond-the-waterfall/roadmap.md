# Beyond the Waterfall: Five-Book Roadmap

| Book | Working Title | Age | World-Glass Piece | Primary Setting | Core Arc & Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Book 1 | **The Three Paths** | 11 | **The Crown Seal** | Glassvale Academy & Bell-Vault | Clara enters Glassvale, chooses Pure Magic, bonds with Maya, Sam, Jorge, and Marissa, and foils Silas's raid on the Bell-Vault. **Secret:** Peter quietly keeps his brass sand-timer close. |
| Book 2 | **The Sky-Forge of Copper Peaks** | 12 | **The Maker Chamber** | Mistmarket & Copper Peaks | Golden clockwork dust vanishes from a mountain sky-forge; Clara and Sam learn why Cold-Iron breaks real Makers' rules while rescuing a trapped glider crew with Jorge. |
| Book 3 | **The Roots of Whisperwood** | 13 | **The Nature Chamber** | Whisperwood & Old Canopy | Living emerald seeds stir beneath the Whisperwood as Blight-thorns choke the Lantern-Fox dens; Maya leads Clara and Marissa to defend the Nature Chamber. |
| Book 4 | **The Cold Star of Ash Marsh** | 14 | **The Starlight Chamber** | Starlight Tower & Ash Marsh | The Hollow Circle breaches the Ash Marsh and captures the Starlight Chamber; Victor narrowly refuses Shadow Magic and warns Monica of an insider near the top. |
| Book 5 | **The World-Glass** | 15 | **The Silver Frame (Final Reassembly)** | Behind the Mistgate / First Vault | **The Peter Reveal:** Peter drops his warm mask at the First Vault with three chambers nearly joined; Clara unites Pure Magic, Jorge's Makers, and Marissa's Nature to remake the World-Glass in balance. |
