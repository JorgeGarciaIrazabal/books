# Marissa (Co-Captain & Keeper of the Living Compass)

- ID: CHAR-0002
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `garbanzo-family` canon rev 3, 2026-11-22 (`DEC-GF-0006`)
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Role and agency
Marissa is the warm, sharp-eyed navigator of human reality in the Garbanzo household. With an instinct for people, teams, and what makes an ordinary Tuesday worth living, she protects the family from trading a hectic city cage for a lonely patch of dirt. She insists on fairness, a 50/50 footing that respects both partners' independence, and a real community where neighbors wave, cafés smell of roasted coffee, and life hums right outside the garden gate.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0001`)**: Punctures Jorge's tendency to fall in love with cheap acreage or flashy spreadsheets with one calm lift of the Compass of Living Streets. Equally quick to hand him a steady cup of coffee when the Amber Terminal drains him dry.
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0003`)**: Co-conspirator in joy, dance steps, warm winter meals, and making sure every big move brings Clara friends, school support, and room to grow.
- **On fairness**: Will not accept vague promises about money or sacrifice; trusts transparent rules on the Brass Scale where every choice shows its real cost (`FACT-0003`).

## Voice and dialogue
Warm, crisp, observant, and dryly funny when cutting through nonsense.

Sample line:
"Lovely pine trees, Jorge. Now show me the bakery, the school bus, and one neighbor who is not a pinecone."

## Stable facts and contested beliefs
- **Accepted fact**: Thirty-eight at quest launch; wields the Compass of Living Streets (`FACT-0006`); loves *cocido madrileño* and lively, finished homes over crumbling renovation traps.
