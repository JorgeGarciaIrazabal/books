# Master Marissa

- ID: CHAR-0003
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glasshaven` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GH-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening onward

## Role and agency
Master Marissa (late thirties) is the Leader Teacher of the Path of Roots (Nature and the Living World). She wants students to slow down enough to hear what wind, stone, and frightened creatures are telling them before taking action. Patient on the outside, she carries fierce protective steel when Hollow Magic threatens the island's living balance.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0001`)**: Meets Clara as a stranger on the first day of term. Her quiet, watchful manner makes Clara nervous at first, yet when shadow magic shakes the tower, Marissa trusts Clara's instincts and teaches her how to breathe through panic.
- **With Maya (`CHAR-0006`)**: Warm, steady mentor who encourages Maya's bond with Pebble the Mossback.
- **With Master Jorge (`CHAR-0002`)**: Dry, affectionate teamwork; she catches what Jorge's machines cannot measure.
- **With Master Peter (`CHAR-0004`)**: Polite and observant; notices her Cloud-Owl ruffling slightly near old Star Spire vaults, though she has no proof of foul play in Book 1.

## Voice and dialogue
Calm, gentle, rhythmic, never wasted, sharp when warning of danger.

Sample line:
"Do not tug the root, Clara. Listen first. The wind always tells you which way the storm is leaning before the rain hits."
