# Sam (Path of Pure Magic, Best Friend)

- ID: CHAR-0003
- Status: accepted

## Visual Identity
Eleven-year-old quirky, funny boy with wild curly ginger hair, crooked round silver glasses, freckles, cozy burgundy knit sweater under an open sleeveless midnight-blue waistcoat with rolled sleeves, carrying a diagram-packed Star-Notebook.

## Role and Agency
Cautious thinker with dramatic comic timing. Sam's spells sometimes wobble teacups or puff silver smoke when he gets rattled, yet he spots hidden patterns, reads old diagrams under pressure, and catches details Clara rushes past.

## Voice and Dialogue
Wry, nervous-funny, sharp-eyed.

Sample line:
"Technically, floating the teacup worked. Nobody said anything about catching the tea."
