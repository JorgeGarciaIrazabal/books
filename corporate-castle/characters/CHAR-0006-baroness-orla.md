## Role and agency
- **Role**: Lead Courtier of Optics in the Tower of Vibes; high practitioner of Illusion Magic and Buzzword Hypnosis.
- **Want**: Empire expansion, executive approval from Lord Sterling and the High Lords, glossy optics, and zero friction—without ever carrying the actual weight of Deep Magic or telling a High Lord no.
- **Fear**: Being exposed as unable to run high-risk ER work, facing Marissa's unblinking Mirror Shield, or absorbing quarter-million-dollar outside-counsel invoices onto her own budget.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Marissa (`CHAR-0001`)**: Desperately tries to pacify and layer Marissa under a new Chief Steward while relying on Marissa's HR Lore and High Lord risk advising. Completely unravels when her Co-Creation Spells and Buzzword Hypnosis shatter against Marissa's flat operational logic.
- **With Doctor Lois (`CHAR-0002`)**: Unstable frenemy alliance—gladly serves as Lois's "velvet human shield" on the Ancient Precedent email because she arrived after last year's restructure, yet panics and offers Lois's org chart without permission, and later blames Lois in front of the Grand Treasurer.
- **With Riley (`CHAR-0009`) and Victoria (`CHAR-0010`)**: Claims the Squires to "empower" them, cancels their one-on-ones, ignores their documentation scrolls, and pressures them to "get to yes" for Courtiers and Warlords.
- **With Ziva (`CHAR-0008`)**: Hires Ziva as Chief Steward to layer Marissa, then flees to St. Barts on December 22 telling Ziva to "lean into the ambiguity."
