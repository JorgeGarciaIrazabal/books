# Series roadmap

## 1. The Ledger of Ashes
A goblin accountant discovers that an audit has hidden a diversion of communal iron. When the book recording wages and debts is seized, she must recover evidence and keep a district fed. Her pursuit exposes a transaction that powerful goblins and Ministry officials both want buried. She can save the original ledger or publish proof of the diversion, but not both. She chooses proof. Her victory pays wages and exposes the immediate fraud; the government's response turns a local fight into a rebellion.

## 2. The Iron Compact (working title)
With a rebellion spreading, Ragna must help sustain an uneasy coalition of goblin workshops and werewolves. Food, shelter and a contested bargain matter as much as battles. A coalition victory carries a cost when one group is asked to surrender its own people. The book resolves that immediate bargain, but the Ministry changes leadership and prepares a harsher campaign.

## 3. The Names We Keep (working title)
Under a new minister, survivors fight over whether to accept a settlement that saves prisoners while erasing the names of the dead. Ragna's apprentice, now an organiser with views of his own, challenges her. The central conflict ends with a costly exchange and a record of the rebellion preserved in more than one community. The larger historical struggle remains unresolved, consistent with later revolts.

Later volumes are plans, not written books. Their events and titles may change. Official sources establish ministerial succession, not these plots or the timing of individual fictional incidents.

## Timeline constraint
Boot, Flack and Gore all held office in 1752, with Flack serving two months. Sequence and duration of the books must accommodate that chronology; the placement of individual leadership changes in books two and three is provisional and may be revised.
