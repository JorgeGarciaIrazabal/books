# Glassvale: World Bible

## Scope and Feel
Glassvale is a hidden magical valley tucked behind a shimmering waterfall curtain called **The Mistgate**, quietly connected to our ordinary world. Its flagship school, **Glassvale Academy**, trains young students from age eleven in three distinct disciplines called **Paths**. The world blends cozy wonder, crisp everyday adventure, and genuine Harry Potter and Narnia style mystery, with easy-to-read English built for reading together with a seven-year-old.

## Geography and Key Places
- **The Mistgate:** A towering waterfall hidden in a rocky mountain gorge. Ordinary passersby see only wet stone and cold spray. Children carrying a waking magical spark (or an academy Pass-Token) feel a warm hum behind the water and step through completely dry into Glassvale.
- **Glassvale Academy:** Perched above terraced waterfalls around a central stone **Bell-Tower** holding three ancient bells (Brass, Leaf, and Star) above a locked stone **Bell-Vault**. Three architectural wings rise around the courtyard:
  - **The Brass Spire (Path of Makers):** Copper domes, turning wind-sails, warm amber workshops, hovering model airships, and blueprint benches.
  - **The Living Dome (Path of Nature):** A soaring glass conservatory woven through an ancient living oak, with drifting indoor rainclouds, mossy rope-bridges, clear streams, and open trails into the Whisperwood.
  - **The Starlight Tower (Path of Pure Magic):** A midnight-stone library and observatory whose stone crown floats six inches above its base. Deep beneath the tower sits the locked wrought-iron **Deep Gate**, sealing away old forbidden chambers of dark magic.
- **The Wider Valley (Expanded Across Books 2 to 5):**
  - **Mistmarket:** A lively lakeside village of glassblowers, clock-tinkers, bakers, and creature-keepers.
  - **The Whisperwood:** An ancient forest where wind carries faint echoes of memories and Lantern-Foxes guide lost travelers.
  - **The Copper Peaks:** Mountain workshops and sky-docks where senior Makers test gliders and storm-catchers.
  - **The Ash Marsh:** A cold borderland far from the academy where old Shadow Magic scars still linger.

## The Three Paths: Mechanics, Personality Traits, and Dark Sides
Every eleven-year-old begins in **Common Term**, learning simple basics of all three Paths before the **Bell-Choosing Ceremony**, when each student chooses their Path.

### 1. Path of Makers (Engineering and Science, led by Jorge)
- **Mechanics:** Runs on blueprints, brass gears, glass spark-vials, careful measurement, and patient trial and error. Makers store steady sparks inside copper coils and glass cells to power gadgets, compasses, mechanical helpers, and protective locks.
- **Strength and Cost:** The most reliable Path. Once tested and fixed, a Maker's device works almost every single time, even in rain or panic. Its limit is preparation: you need parts, tools, and testing rather than instant miracles.
- **Typical Personality Traits:** Practical, witty, stubbornly curious, loves taking things apart to see why they broke, proud of failed drafts that teach the next fix.
- **Dark Section (Cold-Iron):** Milder than Shadow Magic. Involves cruel or greedy contraptions that drain spark from living things or push coils past safe limits. Strictly forbidden, though physical machines can still be switched off or dismantled.

### 2. Path of Nature (Living Balance and Teamwork, led by Marissa)
- **Mechanics:** Runs on quiet listening, patience, rhythm, and real teamwork with weather, plants, streams, and magical animals. A Nature student never bullies a storm or orders a creature around; they ask, tune in, and work together.
- **Strength and Cost:** The most balanced and grounded Path. Skilled students can calm fierce squalls, coax roots into living bridges, track clues on the wind, and soothe frightened beasts. Its limit is trust: rushing, anger, or selfishness breaks the bond immediately.
- **Typical Personality Traits:** Observant, outdoorsy, fiercely loyal, calm under pressure, quick to notice small shifts in weather or people's feelings.
- **Dark Section (The Blight):** Milder than Shadow Magic. Involves forcing roots to choke, twisting gentle weather into biting sleet, or caging creatures through fear. Living things naturally resist The Blight, limiting how far it spreads without outside help.

### 3. Path of Pure Magic (Raw Wonder, Mystery, and Power, led by Peter)
- **Mechanics:** Runs on will, imagination, spoken star-shapes, and raw spark drawn from the invisible starlight woven through Glassvale. Even senior teachers admit Pure Magic holds deep unsolved mysteries about where its power begins and ends.
- **Strength and Cost:** The most powerful Path. It can lift heavy stone, weave shimmering shields, mend shattered glass, or unlock ancient seals in a heartbeat. Its cost is control: panic, pride, or shortcuts cause spells to surge, crack, or rebound dangerously.
- **Typical Personality Traits:** Bold, imaginative, big-hearted, intense, drawn to mysteries, and sometimes tempted to skip slow steps.
- **Dark Section (Shadow Magic):** The most dangerous dark force in Glassvale. When a Pure Magic caster reaches into the cold dark beneath starlight for easy power, Shadow Magic answers by draining warmth, snuffing candles, waking creeping shadow creatures, and slowly hardening the caster's heart.

## The World-Glass and The Hollow Circle
- **The World-Glass:** An ancient three-chambered hourglass forged by the academy's founders. Its three joined chambers held **Golden Clockwork Dust** (Makers), **Living Emerald Seeds** (Nature), and **Liquid Starlight** (Pure Magic). Together they powered the Mistgate's hidden barrier and kept the three Paths in balance. Centuries ago, after a greedy scholar tried to turn it alone to drain the other Paths, the founders split it into **five hidden pieces** (the Crown Seal, the Maker Chamber, the Nature Chamber, the Starlight Chamber, and the Silver Frame) locked behind tests that require all three Paths working together.
- **The Hollow Circle:** A secretive group of Pure Magic extremists who believe Makers and Nature weaken true magic. Across five books, they quietly hunt the five pieces of the World-Glass to reassemble it with Shadow Magic and rule both Glassvale and the world beyond the waterfall.
- **Strict Series Spoiler Rule:** **Peter**, the warm and trustworthy Leader of the Path of Pure Magic, is secretly a member of The Hollow Circle. His small antique brass sand-timer acts as a quiet seeker dial for World-Glass resonance. Readers must NOT discover Peter's true allegiance until Book 5.

## Magical Creatures and Everyday Objects
- **Bramble-Owl:** Fluffy moss-feathered owl-fox hybrid with bright amber eyes and a bushy russet tail; senses storms and dishonest voices (Marissa's companion is **Pip**).
- **Glow-Frog:** Pocket-sized bright turquoise tree-frog whose throat glows warm gold near rain or hidden illusions (Maya's companion is **Pebble**).
- **Lantern-Fox:** Soft golden forest fox whose tail curls with warm lantern-light to guide travelers through mist.
- **Clock-Beetle:** Palm-sized brass-and-glass beetle built by Makers students to carry folded messages and light dark halls.
- **Whisper-Moths (Shadow Magic):** Pale violet shadow-moths that drink candle flames, slip through keyholes, and mimic familiar voices to lure students off path.
- **Ink-Hounds (Shadow Magic):** Silent smoky shadow-wolves with cold emerald-violet eyes; they recoil from clear starlight and the pure chime of the academy bells.
- **Everyday Objects:** **Pass-Tokens** (smooth river-glass coins for crossing the Mistgate), **Bell-Pins** (worn after choosing a Path), **Spark-Vials** (Makers power jars), **Star-Notebooks** (diagram books that glow faintly at night), and **Headmistress Monica's "Second-Best" Collection** (she famously buys the *second* most expensive item of everything: tea sets, silver robes, chairs, and carriages).
