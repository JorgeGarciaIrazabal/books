# Chapter 5: Victor's Shortcut

Wednesday afternoon brought outdoor shield drills in the flagged courtyard, and Victor arrived wearing his sharpest smirk.

"Watch how fast a real spell finishes," Victor muttered to two older students as Peter walked across the yard to check the chalk rings.

Clara noticed a pale purple smudge along the corner of Victor's rolled-up parchment scroll. Before she could call out a warning, Victor stepped into the center ring, raised his leather-gloved hand, and read three jagged words aloud that sounded nothing like Starlight Tower lessons.

The warm afternoon sun dimmed as if a cloud had swallowed it. Cold gray frost raced across the courtyard stones, straight up the fountain wall, and wrapped around two carved stone sparrow statues perched above the archways.

Both stone birds snapped their heads sideways with glowing green eyes, spread heavy granite wings, and shrieked down at the courtyard.

"Duck!" Peter shouted, sprinting across the tiles as students scattered.

One stone sparrow dived straight for Sam, who tripped backward over his own satchel. Maya grabbed Sam's waistcoat collar and hauled him sideways, while Pebble flashed gold from her vest pocket, yet the second stone bird banked sharply around the fountain right toward Maya's head.

Victor stood frozen in shock, his smug smile wiped clean off his face as the dark scroll burned to cold gray ash between his gloves.

Clara did not shout or panic. Inside her head, she heard Marissa's quiet voice: *listen first, then hold steady*. She planted both boots firmly on the cold stone, raised both hands, and wove a clear dome of violet-gold starlight right over Maya and Sam.

The stone sparrow slammed headfirst into Clara's glowing shield with a teeth-rattling *CLANG!* Sparks showered over her hair, pushing her back half a step, yet the dome held firm.

"Keep it steady, Clara!" a crisp voice boomed from the Makers balcony above.

Jorge leaned over the iron rail beside a coiled brass launcher, cranked a lever twice, and fired a shimmering woven copper snare-net straight across the courtyard. The heavy net wrapped cleanly around both thrashing stone sparrows in midair and hauled them clattering onto the grass.

An instant later, Peter reached the fountain, swept one strong hand through the air in a calm wide arc, and called down bright warm gold light that melted the frost and turned the statues back to quiet gray stone.

"Are you hurt?" Peter asked immediately, kneeling beside Clara, Maya, and Sam with deep concern in his eyes while his tall frame shielded them from the lingering chill. "That shield saved Maya and Sam from a dreadful hit, Clara."

Victor stared at the ash on his gloves, white-faced and silent, as Monica stepped briskly onto the courtyard steps with her silver spectacles perched firmly on her nose.

"My office, Victor," Monica said in a voice sharper than winter wind. "And Clara, Maya, Sam, come upstairs once you catch your breath."

Ten minutes later, inside Monica's sunny circular office filled with neat bookshelves and her second-most-expensive velvet armchairs, the Headmistress poured four cups of honey-lemon tea and locked the heavy oak door.

"Victor found that cursed scrap tucked inside his satchel this morning," Monica said quietly. "Whisper-Moths carried it in. Shadow Magic tempts proud casters with easy shortcuts, and it nearly cost us dearly today."

"Why would someone help a Whisper-Moth sneak into Glassvale?" Sam asked, gripping his teacup with both hands this time.

Monica walked to a wall cabinet and tapped a brass plate, revealing a carved wooden model of an ancient three-chambered hourglass. Inside the carving sparkled tiny painted flecks of gold dust, green leaves, and silver stars.

"Long before any of us were born," Monica told them softly, "the three founders forged **The World-Glass**. One chamber held Makers clockwork dust, one held living Nature seeds, and one held Pure Magic starlight. Together, it guarded the Mistgate and kept every Path in balance. When a greedy wizard tried to seize it alone, the founders broke it into five hidden pieces."

Clara leaned forward on the edge of her chair. "And one of those pieces is here?"

Monica nodded gravely. "The **Crown Seal** sleeps inside the Bell-Vault directly beneath our central tower, protected by locks from all three Paths. Someone calling themselves **The Hollow Circle** sent that gray-coated intruder to steal it. Until we find out how he crossed the Mistgate, stay close to your teachers, and never go near the Bell-Tower alone."

Clara glanced toward the tall window. Outside, storm clouds were already gathering blue-black over the mountain waterfalls.
