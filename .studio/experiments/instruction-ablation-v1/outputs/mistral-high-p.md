“It’s booked until 12:20,” Rafi said. “Then the other group.”

“I know.” Leena set her shoulder against the doorframe. “Pia has an audition. Fifteen minutes. Due at 12:30.” She did not say please. She did not say Rafi. She looked at the key on the desk.

Rafi waited for her to ask for him. The fluorescent light hummed. Pia’s shoe scuffed the floor, once, then stopped.

“We could do it quick,” Leena said. “Just the room. Not forgiveness.”
