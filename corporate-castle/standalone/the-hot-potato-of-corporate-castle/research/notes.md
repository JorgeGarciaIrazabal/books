## Key Continuity Delta Between Tabs 2–3 and Tab 4
1. **Cold Alchemy removed**: Tab 2 Ch 1 frames Lois's mutual-separation / hush-gold calculation as Cold Alchemy; under Tab 4, Lois practices **Deep Magic (Institutional Insulation)** and works in genuine sync with Marissa on ordinary risk until pointing upward at a High Lord costs political capital.
2. **New Fracture beat**: Tab 4 adds the Bullying High Lord Formal Inquiry where Lois folds to High Table politics and pushes Marissa to downgrade Okta Excommunication to *coaching*.
3. **Revised Sarai Resignation Showdown**: In Tab 2 Ch 2, Sarai told Orla directly and Marissa emailed Orla two minutes later asking for severance. In Tab 4, Sarai's resignation triggers Doctor Lois summoning Marissa into a Zoom lair (fishing cast -> Mirror Shield -> botched "Interim Captain" Illusion Magic -> operational kill shot about Orla backfilling Sarai -> Marissa refusing an immediate call with Orla and hanging up on Lois).
4. **High Lady Lexi's mystery**: Tab 4 sharpens Lexi's departure around something she saw beside **the King's** throne, teased via a Chapter 2 breadcrumb and paid off at midpoint or Act Three.
5. **Lexicon drift in Tab 3**: Uses older terms (*Dungeon of Employee Relations*, *scroll-box*, *medical leave*, *licensed attorney*, *Senior Employee Relations Investigator*, *SRSOC*) and brief present-tense interludes.
