# Production and packaging

## Edition
Title/subtitle, author/pseudonym, language, imprint, edition/date, formats.

## Front and back matter
Copyright, dedication, contents, acknowledgments, notes, bibliography, author bio,
series order, and other material actually needed.

## Cover and illustrations
Art-direction brief, series identity, image descriptions, asset sources and rights.
Use actual printer/platform specifications for dimensions, spine, bleed, and colors.

## Reader-facing copy
Blurb, description, audience, accurate categories/keywords; no invented endorsements.

## Permissions and provenance
Quote/image permissions, attribution, licensing, and required disclosures.

## Release checklist
Current reviews and accepted exceptions; source/export manifest; proof of actual
output; unresolved issues; author publication decision. Assembly is not publication.
