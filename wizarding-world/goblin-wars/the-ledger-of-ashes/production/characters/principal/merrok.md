# Merrok — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Bank official. Manager of the London accounts.
**Age / build:** Goblin man, fifties, 4 ft 9, immaculate and composed.
**Story function:** The bank official who signs the seizure order and offers an accommodation. He is part of the system Ragna has to move, not a cackling villain.
**Canon anchors:** He manages the London accounts; he has a way of holding his hands behind his back that makes his fingers look broken; he signs the order; he carries a leather case; he is immaculate.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Hands clasped behind his back; fingers angled so they look broken.
- Silver spectacles, always.
- Plain gold ring on the left hand.
- Immaculate dark frock coat, cravat, watch chain.
- No hat, no cloak, no wand.
- Expression is courteous and controlled, not gleeful.

## Avoid

- A greedy grin, rubbing hands, or a villain’s cape.
- Hooked nose, exaggerated rings, or ostentatious jewelry.
- A wrinkled or dusty coat.
- A comic bureaucrat caricature.
