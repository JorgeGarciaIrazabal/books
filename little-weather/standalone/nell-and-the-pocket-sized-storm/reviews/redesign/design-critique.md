## Why the current edition feels assembled

Every picture occupies the same small centered rectangle after the prose. The plan explicitly specifies “after page text” and a 2.5-inch maximum height throughout. Consequently the enlarged cloud on page 9 and enormous sky on page 24 have no greater visual weight than the boots on page 30. Page 17 compresses seven participants into the same footprint. Much of pages 2, 9 and 27 remains empty below the illustration, while the image itself has cramped details. These are design findings supporting the author's objection, even though the rectangular images are internally attractive.

The repeated header rule, abbreviated title, chapter label and divided footer rule reinforce a document template. Page 1 puts both title and prose high on the page, leaving a large unstructured lower field. White space should convey anticipation or intimacy through deliberate balance, rather than recur as leftover capacity.

## Typography and acceptance

Keep comfortable body size, left alignment and generous leading. Reduce running furniture to discreet page numbers; chapter openings need clear hierarchy, not repeated rules. On page 1, give the title more breathing room and compose title/prose as one balanced vertical arrangement. Preserve the surprise until page 2; add no extra illustration or blank page. Text-only pages should use deliberate vertical positioning without decorative filler.

Preserve 30 pages, unchanged prose and two pictures per chapter. Proof all twelve rebuilt pages individually and in sequence: silhouettes must dissolve cleanly into paper, text must remain clear over sky, panorama crops must preserve narrative details, and adjacent compositions must vary. Review actual assets and final pages independently. No canon changes proposed; no print-readiness or rights clearance claimed.
