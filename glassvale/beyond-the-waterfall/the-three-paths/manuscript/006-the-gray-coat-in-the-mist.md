# Chapter 6: The Gray Coat in the Mist

By midnight, wind rattled every windowpane in Glassvale Academy, and thunder rolled down from the peaks like crashing carts.

Clara woke to a sudden, urgent scratching against her bedside table. Sitting on her pillow was Maya's bright turquoise frog, Pebble, glowing so fiercely gold that Clara could see every raindrop streaking down the glass.

Seconds later, her door eased open. Maya stood in the hallway with muddy boots already laced, holding Sam by his sweater sleeve while Sam clutched his Star-Notebook.

"Marissa went down to secure the conservatory roof, Jorge is in the Makers cellar, and Peter rode up to the mountain Mistgate to check the storm-wards," Maya whispered fast. "Every lantern around the Bell-Tower just went dead at once. Look out the arch!"

Clara leaned into the cold corridor window. Across the rain-whipped courtyard, the central Bell-Tower stood pitch black. Pale shapes swirled around its high belfry windows like snow: dozens of Whisper-Moths choking out the storm-lanterns so no warning bell could summon the valley.

"If Silas takes the Crown Seal while the teachers are split up," Sam stammered, adjusting his wet glasses, "the whole valley barrier could crack!"

"Go wake Monica," Clara told Sam, pulling her slate-blue cloak tight over her shoulders. "Maya and I will watch the Bell-Tower door so he cannot slip out."

"Wait," Sam said, squaring his shoulders despite his shaking knees. "Two pairs of Pure Magic eyes are better than one. I will send a spark-flare toward Monica's tower from the bridge, then come right down with you."

Together they dashed across the streaming cobblestones. Sam fired a high bright violet flare up toward Monica's tall window before sprinting after Clara and Maya through the open stone doorway of the Bell-Tower.

The grand ground hall lay silent and icy cold. The three founding bell ropes hung down from the belfry loft beside a wide stone staircase, ending right above an open iron trapdoor in the center of the floor.

Clara raised a cautious orb of violet-gold light and led the way down narrow spiral stairs into the circular Bell-Vault.

Halfway across the vaulted room stood **Silas**, his rain-dark gray coat dripping mist onto the carved floor. Behind him crouched two huge, smoky wolf-shaped creatures made of swirling black ink with cold emerald-violet eyes: **Ink-Hounds**.

At the center of the room rose a stone pedestal holding a shimmering dome of layered light. Two outer iron and root locks lay snapped open beside broken Cold-Iron clamps and dried Blight-pins. Only the shimmering Pure Magic cage still guarded a small, gleaming golden crown-cap pulsing quietly inside.

Silas turned slowly, his silver-etched mask catching Clara's faint starlight.

"Well," Silas murmured in a voice as smooth and cold as wet stone. "Two brave little moths wandered into the cellar. One Maker lock broken. One Nature lock broken. Now only Pure Magic remains."

Maya stepped forward with a pocket full of dried storm-seeds, yet both Ink-Hounds snarled soundlessly and surged forward, cutting off the stairs behind them.

"Wait!" Silas called softly, lifting one gloved hand to pause the beasts an inch from Sam's heels. "They do not need to bite, child. One bright Pure Magic burst from you against that central dome will crack the final lock. Break it for me, and you and your friends walk out untouched."

Clara looked at the gleaming Crown Seal, then at the two cold-eyed Ink-Hounds crouching inches from Maya and Sam. If she fired at the dome, Silas got everything. If she froze, the hounds would rush them.

Then she looked straight up above Silas's head, where an enormous brass-and-crystal prism chandelier hung unlit from the vault ceiling.

"Maya! Cover your eyes!" Clara yelled.

Clara pointed both palms straight upward and poured every ounce of bright, clean Pure Magic starlight she had straight into the crystal prisms.

Blinding white-gold brilliance exploded across the stone vault like noon sunshine inside a cave. The Ink-Hounds shrieked silently, curling backward as the clear light scorched their smoky hides.

"Run!" Clara shouted, grabbing Sam's arm while Maya scooped Pebble safely under her vest.

All three kids bolted back up the spiral staircase into the ground hall just as Silas hurled a blast of frost behind them. Black ice slammed across the outer courtyard door, sealing their escape, while smoky Ink-Hounds surged up out of the vault trapdoor. Driven upward step by step, Clara, Maya, and Sam scrambled into the storm-dark belfry loft overhead and slammed the heavy wooden belfry hatch, only for Silas's frost to freeze the catch shut beneath their boots.
