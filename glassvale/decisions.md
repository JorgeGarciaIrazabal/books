# Glassvale Universe Decisions

## DEC-GV-0001 (2026-11-27) — Initial Universe, Series, and Cast Canon
- **Decision:** Establish the **Glassvale** universe, the five-book series ***Beyond the Waterfall***, and Book 1 ***The Three Paths*** (nine short, heavily illustrated chapters in easy-to-read English for seven-year-old Clara with Harry Potter and Narnia style action and spookiness).
- **Locked Names and Roles:**
  - Academy: **Glassvale Academy** (hidden behind the Mistgate waterfall).
  - Three Paths: **Path of Makers** (Jorge), **Path of Nature** (Marissa), **Path of Pure Magic** (Peter).
  - Academy Leader and Clara's mentor: **Monica** (adult Headmistress who always buys the second most expensive version of everything).
  - Core Student Trio: **Clara** (11, Pure Magic, straight light-brown hair with bangs), **Maya** (11, Nature, sporty, turquoise Glow-Frog Pebble), **Sam** (11, Pure Magic, funny ginger-haired friend with crooked glasses). Makers students (Nora, Ben) remain helpful secondary classmates alongside Jorge.
  - Antagonists: **Victor** (12, proud Pure Magic rival), **Nora** (11, competitive Makers classmate), **Silas / The Gray Coat** (Book 1 Hollow Circle intruder).
  - Overarching Artifact: **The World-Glass** (five hidden pieces across five books).
  - Secret Book 5 Twist: **Peter** (tall, fit, athletic Black man who looks completely warm and trustworthy) is secretly a member of **The Hollow Circle**. Strictly concealed from readers in Books 1 to 4.
- **Visual Direction:** Painterly watercolor, gouache, and crisp ink linework with generous calm open negative-space zones painted directly into illustrations so story text blends into the artwork. Generated in 2x2 batches via Codex `gpt-6.2-sol` (with explicit author-approved fallback to Antigravity's built-in `image-generator` in 2x2 batches if Codex hits quota).
- **Author Independence Note:** All characters, traits, and lore are freshly invented for Glassvale without borrowing from other books in this library.
