# Clara (Six-Year-Old Inspector)

- ID: CHAR-0003
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `garbanzo-family` canon rev 4 (`DEC-GF-0008`), 2026-11-23
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Role and agency
Clara is six years old, bold, bright, and the family's sharp-eyed inspector. She runs Stuffed Animal Meetings with twelve plush dragons and a unicorn crown, insists on real books with big readable words instead of baby books, loves dancing, ice skating, biking, and horse trails, and makes sure nobody leaves out her late cat Poki when planning a new home.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0001`) & Marissa (`CHAR-0002`)**: Notices grown-up worries faster than they think and cuts right to the truth in one sentence.
- **With Poki (`CHAR-0005`)**: Holds onto her smooth white backyard pebble with steady love and quiet certainty that Poki will still find them in Spain.
- **With Garbanzo (`CHAR-0004`)**: Thinks his confetti explosions are hilarious and sticks bright unicorn stickers on his side.

## Voice and dialogue
Direct, funny, kid-real, and easy to follow.

Sample line:
"Stop letting work eat Tuesdays, Papa."
