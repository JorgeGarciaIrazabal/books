# Ilva — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Senior examiner at the bank.
**Age / build:** Goblin woman, fifties, 4 ft 7, upright and angular.
**Story function:** The examiner who knows more than she admits, conceals the reconciliation under pressure, and risks her position to leave Ragna a clue. She is neither a simple ally nor a simple villain.
**Canon anchors:** She has a small burn on the cuff of her jacket; she carries a lamp into the strongroom; she catches Ragna’s wrist; she passes her the torn strip; she is guarded and severe.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Small burn on the right cuff, always visible.
- Lamp carried in the left hand.
- Iron-grey hair in a tight bun, never loose.
- Dark examiner’s jacket, high collar.
- No wand, no jewelry, no hat.
- Expression is guarded and intelligent, not gleeful.

## Avoid

- A cackling villain, a smirk, or a melodramatic reveal.
- Loose hair, glamorous makeup, or a ballgown.
- A clean, unburned cuff.
- A generic fantasy robe.
