# Maya (Path of Nature, Best Friend)

- ID: CHAR-0002
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Visual Identity
Eleven-year-old sporty, sun-warm Black girl with bouncy dark curls tied back with a bright mustard-yellow bandana, bright wide grin, olive utility vest over a sunny ochre-striped shirt, rolled adventure trousers, muddy hiking boots, woven seed-pouch, and her pocket-sized bright turquoise Glow-Frog **Pebble** perched on her wrist or shoulder.

## Role and Agency
Fearless outdoorswoman with zero interest in standing still. Maya hates stuffy rules, reads wind and animal behavior faster than anyone in first year, and anchors the trio's courage when things get spooky.

## Voice and Dialogue
Brisk, sunny, practical, and fearless.

Sample line:
"Pebble just turned bright gold. That is not regular fog, Clara."
