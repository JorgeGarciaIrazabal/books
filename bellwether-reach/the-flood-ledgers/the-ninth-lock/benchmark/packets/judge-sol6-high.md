Reader promise: a fair civic mystery in which paperwork has physical consequences and saving something means choosing what may be lost. Sera Venn discovers that the same lock protection was legally sold to both basins. She has until the tide to expose how and choose a defensible action.

# Fixed canon
- FACT-0001: North Basin and South Basin share the Ninth Lock. Only one basin can receive its protection during a given high tide; changing the assignment after the warning bell takes forty minutes.
- FACT-0002: The next high tide is at 18:00. The warning bell is at 17:30. Walking from the Registry to the Ninth Lock takes twenty minutes; no faster transport is available in the test scene.
- FACT-0003: Sera Venn is a licensed lock inspector. Her left wrist is broken and splinted; she cannot grip with that hand. She carries one brass inspection key in her right coat pocket.
- FACT-0004: Ivo Renn is the Registry clerk and Sera’s estranged former apprentice. He legally registered two incompatible protection certificates. His motives and any coercion remain unknown to Sera and the reader at the opening.
- FACT-0005: The two certificates have the same authentic Registry seal. The seal proves registration, not truth or available capacity.
- FACT-0006: Sera may freeze a disputed assignment only by surrendering her inspector’s license pending a public hearing. A freeze preserves the current assignment; it does not protect both basins. North Basin currently has protection.
- FACT-0007: Sera’s brother lives in North Basin. South Basin contains the city’s communal seed store. Either loss matters; no undisclosed gate, spare pump, miracle, or secret third basin can protect both before this tide.
