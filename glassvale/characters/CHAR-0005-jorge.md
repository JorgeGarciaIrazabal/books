# Jorge (Leader Teacher, Path of Makers)

- ID: CHAR-0005
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Visual Identity
Witty, energetic inventor in his late 30s or early 40s with tousled dark hair, friendly beard stubble, brass magnifying goggles pushed up on his forehead, rolled sleeves, copper-and-leather workshop vest stuffed with brass calipers, screwdrivers, and glowing spark-vials.

## Role and Agency
Demands patience, measurement, and honest trial and error. At the start of Book 1, Jorge has little patience for flashy Pure Magic shortcuts and keeps Clara at arm's length. When Clara gets soot on her hands and helps him debug a sabotaged Brass Spire pump step by step, he becomes one of her fiercest allies.

## Voice and Dialogue
Crisp, upbeat, hands-on, quietly proud when a student tests an idea twice.

Sample line:
"A magic burst is lovely until the spring snaps in the dark. Measure it twice, then light it."
