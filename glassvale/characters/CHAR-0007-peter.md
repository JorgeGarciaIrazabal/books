# Peter (Leader Teacher, Path of Pure Magic)

- ID: CHAR-0007
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through Book 5

## Visual Identity
Tall, fit, athletic, broad-shouldered Black man in his 40s with a trim waist, neat short hair and tidy beard, warm reassuring smile, kind crinkling eyes, tailored deep-plum and antique-gold academy robes. Often carries a small antique brass-and-glass sand-timer.

## Role and Agency
- **Visible Role (Books 1 to 4):** Steady, encouraging, noble protector of Pure Magic students who rescues kids from surges and inspires awe without arrogance.
- **SECRET (Book 5 Reveal Only):** Secretly works with **The Hollow Circle** to locate and reassemble **The World-Glass**. His antique brass sand-timer subtly tilts toward nearby World-Glass pieces. **NEVER reveal or hint overtly at his guilt in Book 1.**

## Voice and Dialogue
Warm, deep, steady, reassuring.

Sample line:
"Easy now, Clara. Fear makes starlight scatter, and pride makes it burn. Breathe, and hold the shape."
