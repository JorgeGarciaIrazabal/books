# Supporting cast — compact character prompts

These are shorter sheets for the wider cast. **Every character block below is standalone.** The positive prompt already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it.

Generate one character at a time. Use the front view as the reference for every later shot.

---

## Fenn

**Role:** Glassworker. One of the three mutual-fund trustees with Sella and Vessa.
**Canon anchors:** Thickened, burn-scarred fingers; carries two stools to the weigh house; his right wrist is injured in the confrontation.

```text
Fenn, a thickset goblin glassworker in his forties, 4 ft 8, ruddy brown skin, broad face, short auburn beard, burn-scarred hands, leather apron over a dark work shirt, thick gloves tucked into his belt. Full-body front, three-quarter and profile views, plus a chest-up portrait, neutral expression, arms at sides.

Neutral warm-grey seamless background, soft even studio light, 1752 historical fantasy, live-action cinematic realism, 35mm film, fine grain, muted umber and slate palette with ember-orange accents, production-design realism, no text.
```

**Negative prompt**

```text
text, letters, watermark, logo, modern clothing, wristwatch, electric light, plastic, cartoon, anime, comic, glossy CGI, superhero pose, neon, lens flare, oversaturated, deformed hands, extra fingers, recognizable Harry Potter characters, Hogwarts, wizarding-world logos, antisemitic caricature, hooked nose, greed imagery, grotesque teeth, duplicate faces, mismatched eyes
```

**Locks:** Burn-scarred hands, leather apron, heavy build. Later scenes: bandaged right wrist.
**Avoid:** Clean unmarked hands, modern glassblowing tools, a posh coat.
