### DEC-HPCC-0005: Lock the Book's Master Spine — The Four Substantiated Findings Against the Two Towers
- **Date**: 2026-11-27
- **Decision**: Lock the book's thematic spine as **Marissa's Final Scroll of Findings against the Two Towers**, organized around strictly **four substantiated findings** (integrating the three core themes, Lois's unmasking phrase, and the Ziva Spellbook motif):
  1. **Finding #1 — The Two-Tower Divide Is a Fraud (Spirit of Deep Magic vs. Institutional Insulation)**: Corporate Castle pretends Liability Tower is austere truth and the Tower of Vibes is pastel spin. In reality, Liability Tower only backs Deep Magic when it is cheap; the moment truth causes **`"High Table discomfort"`** (reserved for unmasking Doctor Lois as a fraud later; keep `"regulatory fire"` in the Prologue) — and again when Lois faces Marissa directly to tell her Sarai has resigned (*"Maybe you already knew,"* *"Interim Captain"*) — Lois folds and reaches for the exact same Illusion Magic as Baroness Orla. Neither tower serves Deep Magic; both serve power.
  2. **Finding #2 — They Minimize the Work They Depend On and Pay for Mistaking the Spellbook for the Gift**: From page one, both Liability Tower and the Tower of Vibes rely on Marissa to do their heavy lifting while systematically minimizing her work and trying to rewrite her history (retroactively branding her "Interim Captain," minimizing her two jobs, rejecting her orderly transition proposal, and denying her severance). **"Having the book isn't the gift"**: they undervalue her, assume she is interchangeable, and pay the price when handing Ziva the gilded title, Spellbooks, and ER Vault keys cannot replace the HR Lore and natural Deep Magic (inverting Chapter 1's unlicensed Squires) that walked out the gate.
  3. **Finding #3 — Free Will Runs Both Ways, and Their Fear of Someone They Cannot Control**: Deep Magic never overrides free will; Marissa cannot force Warlords, High Lords, Lois, or Orla to choose integrity. Corporate Castle mistakes that restraint for submission (*"they always stay"*). Deliberately explore the **fear** both towers feel when they realize they cannot control Marissa because her loyalty is to **truth and justice, not politics** — and ultimately when she exercises her own free will and walks away cleanly on her own terms.
  4. **Finding #4 — The Verdict (History Cannot Be Rewritten Out of Convenience)**: Neither tower is worthy of true Deep Magic. Marissa chooses truth, real people, and the spirit of Deep Magic over rank and executive comfort, and **this book itself stands as her final Scroll of Findings against both towers**, sealing the unspinnable record into Ancient Precedent where no Illusion Magic can touch it.
- **Authority**: Explicit author instruction (`2026-11-27`).
