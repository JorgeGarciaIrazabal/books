# Master Jorge

- ID: CHAR-0002
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glasshaven` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GH-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening onward

## Role and agency
Master Jorge (late thirties) is the Leader Teacher of the Path of Spark (Engineering and Science). He wants students to respect careful testing, honest checklists, and physical limits so no one gets hurt by flashy magic shortcuts. He fears reckless Star-casters blowing open old secrets before they know how the world works.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0001`)**: Has zero prior connection to Clara when she arrives. At first he treats her like every other impatient first-year and grades her test-bench failures bluntly. As Book 1 unfolds, he sees her honesty under pressure and becomes a gruff, dependable guardian who backs her up when she needs real answers.
- **With Leo (`CHAR-0005`)**: Proud yet exasperated mentor trying to keep Leo's sparks from singeing the workshop ceiling.
- **With Master Marissa (`CHAR-0003`)**: Quietly respects her calm judgment; the two teachers share unspoken worry about strange leaks near the Star Spire.
- **With Master Peter (`CHAR-0004`)**: Friendly colleague who respects Peter's strength while privately disagreeing with rushing young Star students too fast.

## Voice and dialogue
Crisp, wry, practical, and grounded in measurements and hands-on fixes.

Sample line:
"Magic that only works when you feel lucky is not magic. It is an accident waiting for Tuesday. Measure it twice."
