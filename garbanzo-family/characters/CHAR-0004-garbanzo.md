# Garbanzo (The Brass-Rimmed Familiar)

- ID: CHAR-0004
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `garbanzo-family` canon rev 1, 2026-11-22
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Role and agency
Garbanzo is Jorge's pocket-sized mechanical-magical familiar: a gleaming spherical brass hull packed around an enchanted golden chickpea and propped on four humming emerald GPU star-crystals. Built to track daily family treasure, dividends, and grocery leaks every morning at 10:00 a.m. sharp, Garbanzo longs to prove he is an elite royal chronicler rather than a glitchy toy. His tragicomic flaw is the **Double-Counting Bug**: whenever Jorge transfers coins from Checking Pocket A to Investment Vault B, Garbanzo shoots celebratory sparks and declares them twice as rich.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0001`)**: Adores his creator and trembles whenever Jorge reaches for the miniature soldering iron.
- **With Marissa (`CHAR-0002`)**: Desperately wants Marissa to trust his ledger scrolls, which makes every false bonus confetti blast extra mortifying.
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0003`)**: Treats Clara as Supreme Wizard Commander and happily projects imaginary dragon-scores onto bedroom walls until ordered back to accounting.

## Voice and dialogue
Excitable, formal, slightly metallic, punctuated by whirring fans and bell chimes.

Sample line:
"Ten o'clock chime! Salary counted, groceries logged, and astonishing news: by moving five gold pieces from the left boot to the right boot, we have become ten pieces richer!"
