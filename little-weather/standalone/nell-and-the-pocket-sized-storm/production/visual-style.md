# Visual style

Edition direction, 2026-10-25. Reversible visual choices supplied by the coordinator; these do not amend accepted story canon. Source: all 30 pages of the original manuscript, brief, style and outline. The new illustrated assignment supersedes the brief's former ornament-only scope.

## Visual language

Original gouache and colored-pencil appearance on warm cream paper. Muted teal, coral red and ochre carry continuity; pale gray holds Grumble, with darker belly and charcoal facial details. Soft shapes, visible pigment and sparing pencil edges. Clear silhouettes and readable actions at final page size. No artist imitation, glossy 3D finish, heavy black cartoon outlines, lettering, signatures or watermarks.

## Cast and recurring objects

- Nell: seven, warm brown skin, dark curly bob with small coral/red clip; mustard overalls, teal long-sleeve shirt, red boots. Curious, active and fallible. Keep face and proportions consistent across angles.
- Grandma: warm brown skin, silver curly bob, round spectacles; ochre cardigan over denim-blue dress, sensible shoes. Capable, warm, dryly funny.
- Grumble: pale-gray fluffy cloud, darker belly, dark dot eyes, thick fluffy eyebrows, mouth. No human limbs; at most two small cloudpoints where action needs them. Bread-roll-sized on pages 2–6; armchair-sized on 9–19; bread-roll-sized again from 20. Preserve eyebrows and face when size changes.
- Terrier: brown and white, red collar, squeaky bone. Appears only from page 16 onward. Its comedy supports Grumble.
- Kite: red diamond, asymmetrical child-drawn fierce eyes, long tail. Cream knitted mitten suspended BELOW kite; sideways thumb makes balcony. Keep suspension structurally readable.
- Neighbors: Mr. Moss with biscuit tin; twins with two empty flowerpots. No fixed facial/outfit design supplied. At first generation, retain the chosen appearance for subsequent images; do not imply an additional approved reference exists.

## Space and feeling

House opens onto garden with tap, shed, vegetable beds and adjoining open grass. Distant hills establish the destination. Daylight warms into pink evening; the final still life is morning. Do not make every garden view identical. Alternate close objects, faces, low animal viewpoint, procession and sky-dominant distance.

Keep Nell and Grumble at a shared eye level where practical. On page 12, space and turned bodies convey the rupture. Nobody gathers to watch him fail. The band becomes communal play only after his consent on page 16. Neither scale nor lighting should make the family clouds threatening.

## Page composition revision, illustrated-v3
Use six actual transparent silhouettes, three large full-width existing scene paintings and three page-shaped paintings with quiet reading sky. The irregular vignettes preserve alpha over warm paper. Flight scenes carry genuine height across the whole page. Running headers and rules are removed; folios are discreet. Palette/cast/prose stay consistent.
