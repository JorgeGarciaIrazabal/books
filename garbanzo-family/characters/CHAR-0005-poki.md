# Poki (The Starlight Cat)

- ID: CHAR-0005
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `garbanzo-family` canon rev 4 (`DEC-GF-0008`), 2026-11-23
- Story-time validity: Quiet memory and light magic across Book 1

## Role and agency
Poki was the family's beloved sleek cat who passed peacefully shortly before the move to Spain (`FACT-0011`), leaving behind a small silver collar bell and a smooth gray stone on the kitchen windowsill. In gentle silver starlight, Poki visits quietly along the New Jersey backyard fence and later along a sunny brick garden wall in Valladolid, completely natural and **strictly costume-free** (`FACT-0010`).

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0003`)**: Answers her biggest secret worry without words, gently tapping her smooth white backyard pebble so she knows Poki will always find them.
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0001`) & Marissa (`CHAR-0002`)**: Quiet reminder not to waste precious family years waiting forever.

## Voice and dialogue
Silent. Expressive through sleek posture, twitching whiskers, soft silver starlight, and a gentle paw tap.

## Stable facts and contested beliefs
- **Accepted fact**: Strictly costume-free (`FACT-0010`); never wears a dragon costume or any costume.
