# Write an article or essay

Accept requests such as “Write an essay about why scenes turn,” “Check the facts
in my post,” or “Give me a clean copy to read.” Ask about audience, central
point, or length only when it would materially change the work.

1. Keep the workspace and brief proportionate. Use the author's collection or
   `blogs/standalone/<slug>/` for a new standalone post. Reuse the studio's
   scaffolding when it supports blogs. Otherwise use the available nonfiction
   template for a factual essay, keeping unused novel records empty.
2. Draft under `manuscript/` and register reading order in `book.json`. Follow
   [the craft guide](../../craft.md) for prose and the author's examples for voice.
3. Have a fresh reviewer read the piece and its brief without the drafting
   rationale. For factual claims, record primary evidence and exact passages.
4. Provide the requested reading file, using the available export tools.
   Check it, update the handoff, and follow the workspace sync policy in
   [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md). Preparing a file and releasing a post are
   separate author requests.
