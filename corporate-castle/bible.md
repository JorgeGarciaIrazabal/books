# Corporate Castle

## Scope and reading experience

Corporate Castle is a satirical corporate-fantasy universe set inside a bustling, glass-walled tech kingdom powered by Slack-runes, glowing Zoom lairs, and the quiet hum of The Equity Vault (`FACT-0001`). Its stories translate the real mechanics of modern corporate governance, employment law, executive politics, and Employee Relations into a sharply observed fantasy-allegory realm where the comedy lands because the institutional logic is dead serious.

At the center of the world sits the quiet, thankless tension between people who actually keep the realm from burning down and the twin towers upstairs that treat truth as either a branding nuisance or a political inconvenience.

## Established constraints

### The Two-Magic System (`DEC-CC-0001`, `FACT-0002`–`FACT-0006`)

Magic in Corporate Castle divides along a strict two-school fault line (superseding the earlier three-school "Cold Alchemy" draft from Tab 1 / `Vocabulary`):

| Discipline | Practitioners | Core Mechanics & Fault Line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deep Magic (ER Watch / Fact-Finding)** | Marissa, Sarai, the ER Squires (Riley & Victoria), High Lady Lexi (retained respect) | Unvarnished truth, objective fact-finding, evidentiary clarity, and applying The Codex equally to frontline guild members and High Lords alike (`FACT-0002`). While Marissa holds both formal licensing and the innate gift, the unlicensed ER Squires are naturally gifted in Deep Magic and intuitively practice it with Warlords every day in an unofficial, frontline way. |
| **Deep Magic (Counsel of Liability / Legal Shield)** | Doctor Lois, Sir Leo, and the Counsel of Liability | An equally valid, necessary branch of Deep Magic focused on statutory counsel, risk analysis, and shielding the realm from legal fire (`FACT-0003`, `DEC-CC-0007`). Nothing is inherently flawed in Liability's discipline—practitioners like Sir Leo wield it with candor (alongside his own personal foibles). **Doctor Lois's** breakdown when Deep Magic must point upward at a High Lord is a **personal failure of courage**, not a defect of Liability's craft (`FACT-0014`). |
| **Illusion Magic** | Courtiers of Optics; High Lady Lexi (vanishing act); Doctor Lois (when bending to politics or cornered) | Lavender-scented jargon, corporate theater, executive revisionism, and hollow remedies (*coaching*, retroactive titles, buzzword hypnosis) used to mask rot and preserve optics (`FACT-0004`). |
| **The Mirror Shield** | Marissa (signature Deep Magic technique) | Pure, unblinking stillness paired with surgical, unspinnable truth. Gives the opponent zero venting, zero soothing, and zero deference, causing political evasion and botched Illusion Magic to rebound directly onto the caster (`FACT-0005`). |

### Hard Rules of Magic (`FACT-0006`–`FACT-0009`)

- **No Coercion (The Two-Way Law of Free Will)** (`FACT-0006`): Neither Deep Magic nor the towers can override free will. The ER Watch cannot force a Warlord or High Lord to act justly; they can only warn, shield, and expose, leaving decision-makers to own their choices. Corporate Castle perpetually forgets the second half of the law: employees possess free will, too. Neither Illusion Magic nor promises locked inside The Equity Vault can chain a person to a kingdom that breaks faith; any soul can walk out the front gates on their own terms, taking their HR Lore with them.
- **Unwritten Magic Evaporates** (`FACT-0007`): Verbal warnings, coaching, and off-the-record legal advice dissolve by sundown unless bound into ink.
- **The Book Isn't the Gift** (`FACT-0008`): Possessing the Spellbooks of Deep Magic or inheriting the keys to the ER Vault does not grant True-Sight or HR Lore.
- **Hush-Gold Limits** (`FACT-0009`, proposed refinement under Two-Magic canon): Even a sound ER ward can be bypassed if leadership chooses to spend hush-gold on a mutual release to silence a statutory curse, though institutional insulation still fails when leadership refuses to pay or refuses to check High Lords.

### ER Watch Spell Roster (Locked Lexicon, Tab 1 + Tab 4)

- **True-Sight**: Cutting through noise, corporate spin, and drama to see the heart of a matter and whatever someone is hiding, regardless of motive.
- **The Mirror Shield**: Marissa's signature Deep Magic defense (stony, unblinking stillness plus surgical operational truth that bounces political evasion and botched Illusion Magic straight back into the caster's lap).
- **The Mirror Ward**: Forcing a Warlord, High Lord, or Courtier to look straight at what their actions are actually accomplishing and the downstream consequences (such as retaliation claims).
- **The Procedural Ward**: Raising the required steps of The Codex (coaching notes, Writs of Warning, Trials by PIP, documentation) before an Okta Excommunication; the spell that gets the ER Watch cursed as "blockers."
- **The Great Incantation**: *"I am only giving you a recommendation. You are the decision-maker."* Strips away imaginary "HR approved this" armor and drops ownership squarely onto the Warlord's signature.
- **The Velvet Snare**: Marissa's signature interview craft for hostile, combative, or evasive witnesses, disarming rage with steady, non-threatening precision and sealing off deflections until contradictions surface on their own.
- **The Credibility Scales**: Marissa's formal instrument for weighing conflicting stories, evidence, and timelines during a Formal Inquiry to substantiate a Crime Against the Codex or clear the accused.
- **Summoning Ancient Precedent**: Drawing on HR Lore to reach into the ER Vault and retrieve Ancient Precedent, anchoring corrective-action or severance recommendations in consistency across ranks.
- **Vault-Sealing**: Locking verified timelines, interview notes, and exhibits inside the ER Vault once a Formal Inquiry closes, feeding future Ancient Precedent so neither tower can rewrite history when a subpoena arrives.
- **The Ink-Bind (Recap E-Parchment)**: Day-to-day survival magic separate from Vault-Sealing; freezes slippery Zoom lair conversations into permanent ink so neither tower can feign amnesia.

## Places, cultures, factions, and systems

### The Twin Spires and the Dungeon (`FACT-0001`, `FACT-0010`–`FACT-0012`)

- **The Tower of Vibes**: A pastel fortress draped in cashmere, ring lights, and lavender-scented buzzwords. Home to the **Courtiers of Optics** (Human Capital partners such as **Brittany** for Sales and **Sienna** for Engineering, later led by **Baroness Orla of Optics**), whose primary craft is enabling and pampering High Lords with Illusion Magic and **The Grimoire of Optics**. Overseen from the shadows by **Lord Sterling, Grand Chancellor of Human Capital**.
- **Liability Tower**: An austere stronghold across the moat from the Tower of Vibes, lined with towering ironbound **Spellbooks of Deep Magic**. Home to the **Counsel of Liability** (**Doctor Lois, Grand Archivist of Liability**, and **Sir Leo, Counsel of Liability**), who practice Deep Magic for institutional insulation against subpoenas and regulatory fire.
- **The ER Dungeon (The Employee Relations Watch)**: Far beneath the pastel lounges of the Tower of Vibes. Home to the **ER Watch**, who triage workplace escalations before they bleed into lawsuits. Houses **The ER Vault**, where all Formal Inquiries, verified timelines, exhibits, and Ancient Precedent are sealed.
- **The Four Fiefdoms**: Engineering (ruled by **Elliot, High Lord of Engineering**), Product, Sales, and Customer Service. Managed day-to-day by frontline **Warlords** (managers) and overseen by **High Lords** (executives) who sit at the **High Table**.
- **Outside Forces**:
  - **The Silk-Robed Sellswords of Outside Counsel**: Expensive external law firm mercenaries hired by Liability Tower when internal capacity collapses (proposed canon from Tab 1 / Tab 3).
  - **The Grand Treasurer of Finance**: Castle financial gatekeeper who cares about gold coin burn rather than tower illusions (proposed canon from Tab 3).

### Core Arcane Artifacts & Institutional Systems

- **The Codex**: The People Guide / Employee Handbook, sworn to in the Hall of Onboarding. Violations are **Crimes Against the Codex**.
- **Spellbooks of Deep Magic**: Ironbound volumes of employment statutes and case law lining Liability Tower, plus Marissa's heavily tabbed personal set.
- **The Grimoire of Optics**: The unwritten HRBP playbook bound in ivory linen and scented with lavender, filled with corporate spin, sparkle emojis, forum shopping, and "vibes mismatch" rebrands.
- **The ER Vault**: The sealed archive of Formal Inquiries, exhibits, and **Ancient Precedent** (past practice).
- **HR Lore**: Unwritten institutional memory living in the heads of the ER Watch; the compass needed to know *which* ghosts exist inside the ER Vault and how to summon them.
- **The Equity Vault**: Holds unvested and vested vault coin; leadership assumes its unlock dates keep practitioners chained to the castle.
- **Communication & Process Instruments**: Slack-runes (Slack), Zoom lairs (Zoom), E-parchment (email), Royal Summons (calendar invite), Documentation scrolls, Severance scrolls, Trials by PIP, Writs / Final Writs of Warning, Okta Excommunication ("excom"), and Protected Exile (medical/protected leave).

## History and competing accounts

- **High Lady Lexi's Departure** (`FACT-0011`, `FACT-0012`, `DEC-CC-0005`):
  - *Accepted objective truth*: Lexi rose from the ER Watch onto the High Lord path until her ER title vanished beneath Vibe Operations and maintained a protective ward over the ER Dungeon. As the years passed, standing that close to the highest court politics proved intolerable to anyone with even a spark of Deep Magic. Disgusted at last, she chose Deep Magic over the climb, secured a private **Offline Oath** from Doctor Lois to shelter the Watch, said a quiet goodbye to Marissa and Sarai, and vanished cleanly the next morning without leaving the upper floors a farewell Slack-rune.
  - *Upper-floor panic*: The Courtiers of Optics panicked the instant her ward fell, refusing to descend into the ER Dungeon to hold the Watch.
- **The Cross-Moat Punt** (`FACT-0013`):
  - *Sterling's spin*: Rebranded as a "temporary cross-tower experiment."
  - *Objective truth*: Lexi's exit left a leadership vacuum in the Tower of Vibes, where not a single Courtier spoke a word of Deep Magic. Caught between her Offline Oath to Lexi and a Royal Volun-telling from Lord Sterling, Doctor Lois was in no position to refuse, expecting a brief loan of a quarter at most. Sterling kicked the ER Watch across the moat to Liability Tower without sending a single gold coin of budget. Left to fend for themselves, Marissa and Sarai found an abandoned wooden barge moored outside Liability Tower's drawbridge and moved the Watch aboard without Lois's help.
