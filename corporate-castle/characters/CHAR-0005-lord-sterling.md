# Lord Sterling, Grand Chancellor of Human Capital

- ID: CHAR-0005
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `corporate-castle` canon rev 1 (`FACT-0013`; Google Doc Tab 4), 2026-11-25
- Story-time validity: Prologue catalyst through Book 1

## Role and agency
- **Role**: Grand Chancellor of Human Capital; The Catalyst. Lurks in the shadows of the Tower of Vibes and treats Corporate Castle's workforce like a quarterly grain ledger.
- **Want**: Zero incremental gold coin spent, zero friction for High Lords, and zero personal accountability when operational messes detonate.
- **Method**: Always felt the ER Watch smelled suspiciously like Liability anyway. The instant Lexi's ward collapsed, he exploited Doctor Lois's Offline Oath to kick the entire ER Watch across the moat as a "temporary cross-tower experiment"—sending zero gold coin of budget and zero authority to hire, promote, or fund them—and melted back into the shadows before the fallout hit (`FACT-0013`).

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Doctor Lois (`CHAR-0002`)**: Smoothly traps her with her own Offline Oath and leaves her holding an unfunded dungeon.
- **With Baroness Orla (`CHAR-0006`, proposed)**: Approves Orla's "zero incremental gold coin" scheme to pull ER back into Vibes and rebrand Sarai's seat into a Chief Steward layering Marissa, so long as it costs nothing and keeps the High Lords happy.
- **With Marissa (`CHAR-0001`)**: Never speaks to her directly in the drafts; dismisses her HR Lore and Ancient Precedent as spreadsheet noise and assumes The Equity Vault guarantees she won't walk away in Q4 (proposed Tab 2/3 detail).

## Voice and dialogue
Breezy, bottom-line, clipped, and casually lethal—neither lavender Vibes theater nor Deep Magic purism, just executive P&L indifference.

Sample line (grounded in Tab 2/3 characterization):
"Spare me the HR lore. Give me the one-sentence version: does Deep Magic force us to hand her gold coin?"

## Stable facts and contested beliefs
- **Accepted fact**: Masterminded the unfunded cross-tower punt of the ER Watch into Liability Tower (`FACT-0013`).
- **Blind spot**: Forgets the second half of the Law of Free Will (`FACT-0006`)—convinced nobody walks away before The Equity Vault unlocks or without a severance scroll.
