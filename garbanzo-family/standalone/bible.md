# Standalone — Garbanzo Family

## Series promise and engine
Standalone family keepsakes, fables, and tall tales set inside the Garbanzo Family universe (`garbanzo-family/bible.md`). Each book delivers a complete emotional and comic adventure grounded in real family milestones while remaining readable aloud together.

## Audience, themes, and tone
Built for Jorge, Marissa, and Clara: playful, theatrical magic with sharp comedy for adults and genuine wonder for a bright six-year-old reader.

## Reading order and entry points
Standalone volumes listed in `series.json` can be read in any order. *The Quest of the Golden Garbanzo* introduces the family, Slow-Simmer Garbanzo Magic, Garbanzo the familiar, and the leap from New Jersey and New York to Spain.
