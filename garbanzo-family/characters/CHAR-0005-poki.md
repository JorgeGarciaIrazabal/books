# Poki (The Starlight Cat)

- ID: CHAR-0005
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `garbanzo-family` canon rev 1, 2026-11-22 (`FACT-0010`)
- Story-time validity: Memory and starlight guardian across Book 1

## Role and agency
Poki was the family's real cat, deeply loved and mourned after crossing the Silver Wall just before the relocation quest (`FACT-0011`). In the Garbanzo Family universe, Poki visits quietly along moonlit fences and garden walls: sleek, dignified, silent, and wearing **no costume whatsoever** (`FACT-0010`). Poki never delivers cheesy speeches; instead, a soft paw tap knocks deceptive real-estate brochures off tables, and a calm silhouette along a sunlit Spanish garden wall seals the family's choice of home.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0003`)**: Gentle anchor of reassurance that love travels across oceans without needing a plane ticket.
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0001`) & Marissa (`CHAR-0002`)**: Quiet reminder of real loss, real time, and why waiting forever is a trap.

## Voice and dialogue
Non-speaking. Communicates through crisp head-tilts, deliberate paw-swats on bad paperwork, and silver-bright quiet presence.

## Stable facts and contested beliefs
- **Accepted fact**: Strictly costume-free (`FACT-0010`); no dragon costumes, hats, or capes ever.
