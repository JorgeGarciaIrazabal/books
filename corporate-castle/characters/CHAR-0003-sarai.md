# Sarai, Captain of the ER Watch

- ID: CHAR-0003
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `corporate-castle` canon rev 1 (`FACT-0010`, `FACT-0012`; Google Doc Tab 4), 2026-11-25
- Story-time validity: Prologue through resignation beat (early Book 1)

## Role and agency
- **Role**: Captain of the ER Watch, directly leading the ER Squires (Riley and Victoria) who hold the frontline line between trigger-happy Warlords and employees across Trials by PIP, Writs of Warning, and daily workplace skirmishes (`FACT-0010`).
- **Want**: To protect her Squires, shield Marissa from Courtier fire drills so Marissa can focus on the ER Vault, and keep her own health and sanity intact.
- **Limits**: Carries an unsustainable operational load in an unfunded dungeon without executive air cover once Lexi's ward collapses; eventually goes out on Protected Exile and decides to resign.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Marissa (`CHAR-0001`)**: Loyal peer, co-carrier of the Watch, and genuine friend. Steps up to take the Courtier advising baton so Marissa can breathe, and genuinely hates leaving Marissa holding the bag when Protected Exile leads to her resignation.
- **With Riley (`CHAR-0009`) and Victoria (`CHAR-0010`)**: Warm, protective captain who shields them from bullying Courtiers and Warlords.
- **With High Lady Lexi (`CHAR-0004`)**: One of the only two people Lexi descended into the ER Dungeon to say goodbye to in person before vanishing (`FACT-0012`).

## Voice and dialogue
Warm, grounded, dryly funny in private Slack-runes, and steady under frontline pressure—the person who drops the blood pressure in the room.

Sample line (grounded in Tab 2/Tab 4 characterization):
"Hand me the baton. You belong in the ER Vault, not refereeing Friday-afternoon firings for Sales."

## Stable facts and contested beliefs
- **Accepted fact**: Leads the ER Squires alongside Marissa; receives Lexi's private goodbye in the Prologue (`FACT-0010`, `FACT-0012`).
- **Book 1 arc trigger**: Her resignation triggers Doctor Lois's panicked 1:1 with Marissa (Tab 4 Showdown) and the Watch's transfer back to the Tower of Vibes.
