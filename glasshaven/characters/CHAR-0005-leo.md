# Leo

- ID: CHAR-0005
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glasshaven` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GH-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 onward

## Role and agency
Leo (11) is Clara's cheerful, motor-mouthed best friend from the Path of Spark. He wants to build gadgets that actually work on the first try (they usually work on the fourth) and proves that clever engineering can stand beside pure magic in a crisis.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Clara (`CHAR-0001`)**: Instant train-ride ally; his practical measuring habits balance Clara's impulse to leap first.
- **With Maya (`CHAR-0006`)**: Teases her turtle gently, then builds a tiny brass shell-warmer for it when the nights turn cold.

## Voice and dialogue
Fast, enthusiastic, full of "what if" tinker ideas.

Sample line:
"Okay, so it smoked a tiny bit. That just means the spring worked twice as fast as I drew it!"

## Physical and historical facts
- Pronouns: he/him. Age: 11.
- Appearance: Messy sandy-blond hair, scattered freckles, bright blue eyes, brass goggles perched on his forehead, copper-trimmed navy vest over rolled sleeves, oil-smudged notebook and glowing brass Spark-Compass in hand.
