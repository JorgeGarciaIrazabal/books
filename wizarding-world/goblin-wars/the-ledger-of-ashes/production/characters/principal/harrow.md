# Harrow — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Ministry wizard. The official who serves the seizure order.
**Age / build:** Human man, fifties, 6 ft 1, tall and narrow.
**Story function:** The face of the Ministry’s authority. He is patient, cold and procedurally immaculate. He does not need to raise his voice.
**Canon anchors:** Long, narrow face; dark travelling cloak; wand tucked into a silver clip at his wrist; carries no case; examines the ledger; shrinks it and takes it; cold patience.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Silver wand clip on the right wrist, always visible.
- Black travelling cloak with a high collar.
- Clean-shaven, dark hair swept back with grey temples.
- No hat, no beard, no jewelry.
- Polished boots, immaculate clothes.
- Expression is patient and cold, not snarling.

## Avoid

- Hogwarts robes, house colours, or a school crest.
- A beard, a top hat, or a flamboyant villain costume.
- Skull motifs, dark smoke, or glowing red eyes.
- A raised wand or a screaming face.
