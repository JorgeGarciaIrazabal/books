# Glasshaven Decisions

## DEC-GH-0001 (2026-11-27) — Author Creative Mandate & Canon Foundation
- **Audience & Reading Style**: Read-together chapter book in plain, engaging English for a seven-year-old daughter (Clara) who loves Harry Potter and Narnia, so prose stays crisp and easy to understand while allowing spooky atmosphere and genuine action.
- **Terminology**: Use **Path** for the three academy disciplines (**Path of Spark** for Engineering & Science, **Path of Roots** for Nature, **Path of Stars** for Pure Magic).
- **Series & Book 1 Structure**: Plan a **5-book** overarching story building book by book. Book 1 (*Clara and the Whispering Bell*) stars **eleven-year-old Clara** across **9 short chapters**, longer than a single bedtime picture book yet brisk enough for cozy read-together sessions, heavily illustrated with open calm spaces inside the art so story text blends onto the page.
- **Cast & Relationships**:
  - **Clara** (11) enters the **Path of Stars** and makes close friends inside Stars and across Spark and Roots (**Leo**, **Maya**, **Sam**).
  - **Master Jorge** leads the **Path of Spark**; **Master Marissa** leads the **Path of Roots**. Clara has **no relationship** with either teacher at the start and builds genuine trust with both during Book 1.
  - **Master Peter** leads the **Path of Stars**. He is a **big, Black, strong man who looks warm and trustworthy**. Secretly (kept strictly hidden from Book 1 readers until the Book 5 finale), he belongs to the antagonistic Quiet Crown faction.
- **Originality**: All characters, creatures, mechanics, and world details are freshly invented for Glasshaven with zero borrowing from other studio universes.
- **Art Workflow**: Generate initial and interior illustrations in **2x2 grids** via Codex `gpt-6.2-sol` to conserve quota, then slice and visually inspect each panel.
