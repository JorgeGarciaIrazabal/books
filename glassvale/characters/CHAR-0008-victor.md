# Victor (Pure Magic Rival, Age 12)

- ID: CHAR-0008
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening onward

## Visual Identity
Sharp, poised twelve-year-old boy with neatly combed pale-blond hair, keen gray-blue eyes, crisp high-collared navy-and-silver jacket with brass buttons, and dark leather gloves.

## Role and Agency
Second-year Pure Magic prodigy who craves spotlight and hates seeing first-year Clara draw praise from Monica and Peter. His hunger for shortcuts leads him to try a Shadow-tainted courtyard scroll in Chapter 5, nearly getting everyone hurt before Clara and Jorge save him.

## Voice and Dialogue
Crisp, smug, competitive.

Sample line:
"Cute spark, first-year. Let me know when it stops shaking."
