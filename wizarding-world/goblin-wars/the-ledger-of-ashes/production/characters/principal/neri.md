# Neri — character prompt sheet

**Role:** Dorrik’s daughter. Hinge-maker and workshop designer.
**Age / build:** Goblin woman, twenties, 4 ft 9, strong shoulders, work-hardened hands.
**Story function:** The daughter who counted the iron, designs the household warning bell, and whose distrust of Ragna follows the disputed accounts.
**Canon anchors:** She wears her father’s leather apron folded twice at the waist; she files a thin metal tongue at the workbench; she has a commission to fit a latch at Blackwater; she is direct and does not soften.
**Design note:** Appearance is a trailer proposal, not manuscript canon.

**Standalone prompts:** every prompt below already includes the shared style, and the negative prompt follows it. Copy one section and send it.

## Continuity locks

- Her father’s leather apron, folded twice at the waist.
- Hammer on the right hip, tool roll on the left.
- Black hair braided and pinned up, never loose.
- Soot on the forehead, not a clean face.
- Heavy leather gloves, worn at the fingers.
- Expression is direct and unsoftened.

## Avoid

- A damsel pose, a glamour shot, or loose flowing hair.
- Modern power tools or safety gear.
- A clean, unused apron.
- A smile that undercuts her suspicion.
