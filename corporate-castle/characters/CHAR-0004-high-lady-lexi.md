# High Lady Lexi of Vibe Operations

- ID: CHAR-0004
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `corporate-castle` canon rev 2 (`FACT-0011`, `FACT-0012`, `DEC-CC-0005`), 2026-11-25
- Story-time validity: Pre-Prologue & Prologue departure

## Role and agency
- **Role**: The Forerunner. Rose from the ER Watch onto the High Lord path as Corporate Castle piled org after org onto her crest until her ER title vanished beneath Vibe Operations (`FACT-0011`).
- **Want / Choice**: Navigated court politics longer and more skillfully than anyone in ER, keeping a protective ward between the ER Dungeon and the Tower of Vibes while Marissa and Sarai carried the Watch below. Over time, standing so close to the upper court proved intolerable to anyone with even a spark of Deep Magic. Disgusted at last, she decided she had served the Court long enough (`FACT-0012`, `DEC-CC-0005`).
- **Final act**: Secured a private Offline Oath from Doctor Lois to shelter the ER Watch if the Courtiers panicked, descended to the dungeon to say a quiet goodbye to Marissa and Sarai, and vanished the next morning without leaving the upper floors a farewell Slack-rune.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Marissa (`CHAR-0001`)**: Foil and forerunner. Lexi chose the High Lord climb while Marissa chose pure Deep Magic (less innate magic implied strictly through their divergent paths, never spelled out bluntly).
- **With Doctor Lois (`CHAR-0002`)**: Trusted Lois enough as a peer to extract an Offline Oath to shelter the Watch, unintentionally handing Lord Sterling the lever he uses to trap Lois.
