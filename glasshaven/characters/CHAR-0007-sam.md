# Sam

- ID: CHAR-0007
- Status: accepted

## Role and agency
Sam (11) is Clara's classmate inside the Path of Stars. Cautious, thoughtful, and fascinated by old star-charts, Sam hates breaking rules yet refuses to let Clara walk into danger alone.

## Voice and dialogue
Careful, earnest, quick to notice small map clues.

Sample line:
"There are only six staircases drawn on the map, Clara, so why is there a seventh cold draft?"

## Physical and historical facts
- Pronouns: he/him. Age: 11.
- Appearance: Neat dark curls, round wire-rimmed glasses, midnight-indigo Star cloak, pocket slate that projects tiny golden map lines.
