# Standalone — Garbanzo Family

## Series promise and engine
Standalone family stories set inside the Garbanzo Family universe (`garbanzo-family/bible.md`). Each book tells a fun, heartfelt, easy-to-follow story about ordinary humans navigating big family milestones with a sprinkle of gentle magic.

## Audience, themes, and tone
Built for Jorge, Marissa, and Clara: light, lively everyday English that is effortless to read aloud with a bright six-year-old while still funny and emotional for grown-ups.

## Reading order and entry points
Standalone volumes listed in `series.json` can be read in any order. *The Quest of the Golden Garbanzo* follows the family's real-feeling journey from New Jersey and Manhattan to a joyful new life in Valladolid, Spain.
