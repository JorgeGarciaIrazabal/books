# Write and revise a book

Accept requests such as “Start a mystery,” “Continue where I left off,”
“Tell me where the opening drags,” and “Make me an EPUB I can read.”
Choose the necessary roles, files, and tools for the author. Follow
[AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and the [studio workflow](../../workflow.md),
including quiet workspace sync at milestones.

- **Start a book.** Identify the reader, premise, intended format, and scope.
  Create the book records from the template or the workspace helper. Make
  reversible assumptions explicit, then draft a representative passage.
- **Continue or revise.** Read the book's current notes and named passages.
  Work on the next authorized scene or section, preserving existing decisions.
- **Review.** Give a fresh reviewer the brief and actual text to assess. Report
  where a reader may lose the thread, with examples and a focused revision.
- **Prepare a reading copy.** Assemble the requested format in the book's chapter
  order and open or inspect the result. Deliver a usable copy and identify any
  consequential limits. A reading copy is for review unless the author asks to
  make it available to readers.

Use `tools/studio.py` for available scaffolding, review packets, checks, and
exports. If the checkout provides Make targets, those are operator shortcuts.
Use the [tooling guide](../../../docs/tooling.md) and current role roster for
tool and model details. Save progress and leave current notes before finishing.
