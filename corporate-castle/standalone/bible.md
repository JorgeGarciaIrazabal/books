# Corporate Castle — Standalone

Standalone container for *The Hot Potato of Corporate Castle* (`DEC-CC-0002`). If the author later expands Corporate Castle into a multi-book series, books can be moved into a named series folder and registered in `series.json`.

## Scope and promise
Sharp, dryly funny corporate-fantasy satire grounded in real Employee Relations, Employment Law, and executive politics—showing what happens when twin towers mistake quiet competence for guaranteed servitude.
