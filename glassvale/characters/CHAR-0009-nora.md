# Nora (Secondary Makers Classmate, Age 11)

- ID: CHAR-0009
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening onward

## Visual Identity
Eleven-year-old Makers student with short copper-brown bob, safety spectacles perched on her nose, and a neat brass-pocketed workshop apron.

## Role and Agency
Competitive, clock-precise Makers student who initially scoffs at Pure Magic messiness ("Pure Magic is just guessing with extra glitter"), yet shows up loyally with a Clock-Beetle and Jorge's instructions when Clara's trio is trapped in Chapter 7.

## Voice and Dialogue
Snappy, exact, no-nonsense.

Sample line:
"Three turns of the brass key, Clara, not four. Four melts the spring!"
