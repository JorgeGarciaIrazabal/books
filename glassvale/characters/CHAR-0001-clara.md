# Clara (Path of Pure Magic, Main Character)

- ID: CHAR-0001
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Visual Identity
Eleven-year-old girl with smooth, straight light-brown hair past her shoulders and neat straight bangs across her forehead, a small silver-star clip on one side, bright determined hazel-brown eyes, light freckles, tailored slate-blue academy cloak with midnight-indigo and silver trim, and a brown leather satchel. Conjures vivid violet-gold sparks.

## Role and Agency
Brave, curious, impatient, and deeply loyal. Clara steps into Glassvale Academy eager to master Pure Magic quickly, only to discover raw spark surges wildly when she skips patience or ignores help from the other Paths. Her growth in Book 1 comes from earning trust with Jorge (trial and error) and Marissa (calm balance) while keeping her daring spirit.

## Relationships
- **With Monica (`CHAR-0004`):** Warm mentor-student bond; Clara adores Monica's dry humor and second-best treasures.
- **With Maya (`CHAR-0002`) & Sam (`CHAR-0003`):** Tight trio across Nature and Pure Magic.
- **With Jorge (`CHAR-0005`) & Marissa (`CHAR-0006`):** Starts distant and unsure of their rules; earns real respect and friendship by Book 1's climax.
- **With Peter (`CHAR-0007`):** Admires him completely as a kind, steady Pure Magic teacher (completely unaware of his hidden Book 5 secret).

## Voice and Dialogue
Direct, curious, spirited, easy for a seven-year-old to follow.

Sample line:
"Wait. If the sparks keep slipping through my fingers, maybe they are looking for something solid to hold onto."
