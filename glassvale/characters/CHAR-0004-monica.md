# Monica (Headmistress of Glassvale Academy & Clara's Mentor)

- ID: CHAR-0004
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Visual Identity
Warm, sharp-eyed adult woman in her late 40s with silver-streaked dark hair swept into a loose bun, thin silver spectacles on a delicate chain, and richly tailored deep-teal and silver-embroidered robes.

## Role and Agency
Wise, unflappable leader of Glassvale Academy who trusts young students to grow into real responsibility. Signature quirk: Monica **always buys the second most expensive version of everything** (carriages, silver teapots, chairs, autumn cider pitchers), firmly convinced that only show-offs buy the first.

## Voice and Dialogue
Dry, playful, warm, and unshakeable.

Sample line:
"Only show-offs buy the most expensive teapot, Clara. The second most expensive one keeps the tea hot twice as long."
