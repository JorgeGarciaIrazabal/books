## Role and agency
Clara is an eleven-year-old newcomer entering Lanternhold Academy's Path of Stars. She wants to prove she belongs in a wondrous, slightly frightening world without losing who she is or letting her friends down. Brave, quick-witted, and fiercely loyal, her greatest weakness is rushing headfirst into danger when she thinks nobody else will act, causing her raw Aether spells to flare out of control.

## Contradictions and relationships
- **With Leo (`CHAR-0005`) and Maya (`CHAR-0006`)**: Deeply protective cross-Path friendship; she learns that her wild Star-magic needs Leo's careful plans and Maya's steady calm.
- **With Sam (`CHAR-0007`)**: Bookish Star-Path classmate who helps Clara slow down and study patterns.
- **With Nora (`CHAR-0008`)**: Tense rivalry in Book 1 when Clara refuses to cheat with shadow shortcuts.
- **With Master Jorge (`CHAR-0002`)**: Starts distant and intimidated by his strict workshop rules; earns his fierce respect and warmth by admitting mistakes and sticking with tough repairs.
- **With Master Marissa (`CHAR-0003`)**: Starts uncertain around Marissa's quiet gaze; discovers steady courage and tenderness together in crisis.
- **With Master Peter (`CHAR-0004`)**: Admires his huge, warm laugh and reassuring confidence; sees him as a wise protector in Book 1.
