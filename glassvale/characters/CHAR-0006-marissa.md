# Marissa (Leader Teacher, Path of Nature)

- ID: CHAR-0006
- Status: accepted
- Canon revision / provenance: `glassvale` canon rev 1 (`DEC-GV-0001`), 2026-11-27
- Story-time validity: Book 1 opening through finale

## Visual Identity
Warm, calm Asian woman in her late 30s with dark hair in a loose side braid woven with tiny green leaves, emerald-and-moss linen cloak clasped with a silver leaf pin, sturdy boots, accompanied by **Pip**, her fluffy moss-feathered Bramble-Owl.

## Role and Agency
Believes true strength comes from stillness, listening, and partnership with living things. Marissa is initially quiet and watchful toward impatient Pure Magic students, including Clara. When Clara slows down to help heal a Blight-wounded root beneath the Living Dome without forcing magic, Marissa welcomes her like family.

## Voice and Dialogue
Calm, gentle, clear, unhurried.

Sample line:
"Stop shouting at the wind, Clara. Ask it where the cold came from, and then listen."
